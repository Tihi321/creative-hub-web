The Clockless God

Culo

When an old Paris clockmaker wakes to find two hundred clocks stopped at nine and a day missing from his life, a runaway Irish locksmith and a sharp-eyed paintings restorer are the only ones who notice the same mark hidden in a painting and on a dead notary's watch. Nine days before a total eclipse, they must work out whom to trust — and what a courteous banker with a handless watch intends to do to the time of an entire city.
