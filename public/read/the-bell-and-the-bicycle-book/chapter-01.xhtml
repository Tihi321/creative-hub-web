The Bell and the Bicycle

Lana's home, early morning. Mama holds out a shiny red bell.

On screen: Lana (yellow dress); Mama (apron, bun).

The bell is small, round and shiny red.

Mama gives it to Lana for her blue bicycle.

Ring-ring, it says when Lana shakes it.

The yellow front door and front garden, morning. Lana fixes the bell to her blue bicycle.

On screen: Lana (red boots); Mama (apron).

Lana turns the little screw tight.

She rings it. Ring-ring!

The blue bicycle is ready.

The park gate, morning. Tomo waits with his green bicycle and Kiki at his feet.

On screen: Lana (braids); Tomo (cap, freckles); Kiki (white ear).

Kiki wags her tail so fast it makes a little wind.

Lana rides up and rings her bell. Ring-ring!

The winding gravel path, morning. Lana and Tomo ride between the big trees with Kiki after.

On screen: Lana (braids); Tomo (cap); Kiki (tongue out).

They ride along the winding path. Ring-ring!

Kiki runs after them, paws going pat-pat.

The bell rings and rings all morning.

The winding gravel path, morning. The front wheel hits a big root and the red bell pops off.

On screen: Lana (startled); Tomo (braking); Kiki (ears up).

The front wheel bumps a big root. Bump!

The red bell pops off and rolls away into the grass.

Lana and Tomo look at the empty handlebar.

The red bench and the big chestnut tree, morning. Lana looks under the red bench and Kiki sniffs the reeds.

On screen: Lana (kneeling); Tomo (squinting); Kiki (sniffing).

They look under the red bench. No bell.

They look in the grass and by the pond.

The ducks watch with round eyes.

The red bench and the big chestnut tree, late morning. Kiki digs in the leaves and they fly.

On screen: Kiki (muddy); Lana (leaves); Tomo (leaning in).

Kiki sniffs the leaves. Sniff, sniff, sniff.

She digs and digs, and the leaves fly up like little birds.

Lana and Tomo lean in close.

The red bench and the big chestnut tree, late morning. Kiki trots out with the red bell.

On screen: Kiki (bell); Lana (arms up); Tomo (freckles).

Kiki trots out with something in her mouth.

It is the red bell! Muddy, but whole.

The children cheer. Kiki wags her whole back end.

The red bench and the big chestnut tree, late morning. Lana wipes the red bell and rings it.

On screen: Lana (bell); Tomo (grinning); Kiki (muddy).

Lana wipes the bell on her sleeve until the red shines again.

She rings it. Ring-ring!

Tomo laughs, and Kiki barks one happy bark.

The winding gravel path, late afternoon. Lana and Tomo ride home in the golden sun with Kiki behind.

On screen: Lana (dress, bell); Tomo (cap); Kiki (trot).

The sun turns gold and the shadows grow long.

They ride home slowly, and the bell rings all the way. Ring-ring, ring-ring.

The yellow front door and front garden, early evening. Mama stands on the step and Lana rings it.

On screen: Mama (apron); Lana (bell); Tomo (cap); Kiki (collar).

Mama asks about the ride.

Lana rings the bell for her. Ring-ring!

Mama smiles. It sounds like a good day.

Lana's home, night. The red bell sits on the nightstand and Kiki curls at her feet.

On screen: Lana (asleep); Kiki (curled up, white ear).

The red bell sits on the nightstand, shiny and quiet.

Lana is asleep, and Kiki curls at her feet.

Goodnight, bell. Goodnight, ring-ring.
