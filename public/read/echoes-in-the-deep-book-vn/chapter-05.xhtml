Cormorant, 09:45

Ambience: near-total quiet, scrubbers whispering, one soft tap through the headphones

Red emergency lamps only. The passive array's green glow paints Lian's face. Neve sits with headphones clamped on and the tablet on her knees. Rivas has the log open and a pen that has not moved in a while. Kofi crouches by the aft bay with a spare beacon open in his lap.

LIAN Commander. It's thinning along the Halcyon's port side. Pulling back off her like a hand off a hot stove.

KOFI Say that again, and this time say it was good.

NEVE It's good for the Halcyon.

RIVAS You've got a voice. What's the rest of it?

NEVE The leading edge has crossed the debris line. An hour ago it was nowhere near.

RIVAS Toward us.

NEVE Yes.

RIVAS How long?

NEVE It's not a bomb. If you want a number: under our keel inside a day.

RIVAS Define under our keel.

NEVE On the hull. Growth meets a hard thing and has three choices. Goes round, goes over, or lifts.

KOFI It can have the other two. I'd like it under the boat least.

RIVAS Then we lift. Hover a hundred metres off the floor and let it pass underneath.

KOFI On surging thrusters. To lift, I use them hard, and hard goes to the frame I taped and strapped. I can give you twenty minutes off the floor, then I set her down and let the strap do its job.

RIVAS And in twenty minutes the thing is still coming.

NEVE It crossed that whole field while we sat doing nothing. Which tells me it was following something. Not only us. I want to know what.

Cormorant, 10:20

Ambience: low pump note, a pencil scratching, the soft wave of taps through headphones

A tablet glows on the console with Neve's rough map of the field. Lian hunches beside it with a stylus. Kofi has the work lamp over the bench.

On screen: Neve (headphones round her neck, pencil in hand), Lian (stylus working the tablet), Kofi (work lamp over the bench), Rivas (at the air figures)

NEVE It isn't a wall. That was my mistake from the start.

LIAN It's a field. Hundreds of them.

NEVE Crowns. Each about the size of a dinner plate, and every one answers every other.

LIAN Crickets in a hedgerow. One starts and the whole hedge is going.

NEVE A travelling wave. And the wave has a direction, and the direction is us.

LIAN Put your phones on. There's something under the taps. Not a note. A rhythm.

RIVAS I can't hear anything.

LIAN Nobody can, the first time. It's the way a cat looks at you before you've made a sound.

KOFI (arriving, wiping his hands) What am I listening for?

LIAN A thump every so often. A beat under all the tapping.

KOFI (a startled laugh) That's a warm engine, that is. Every battery in this boat is a little coal fire going out through steel.

KOFI If I were a crystal in a cold trench, I'd sniff out the warmest thing and grow toward it.

NEVE The battery bay's under the galley floor.

KOFI It is. And it's the warmest thing for a hundred kilometres in any direction.

RIVAS So it's not chasing a rescue. It's chasing our batteries.

NEVE It's chasing warmth and it comes toward rhythm. Neither is malice, Commander. Both are appetite, and appetite can be baited.

RIVAS Bait can be eaten.

LIAN It didn't eat the Halcyon. It came, it answered, and it left. That's not eating. That's investigating.

Cormorant, 10:52

Ambience: a quiet, faint tapping through the headphones

Rivas works the outside camera on its lowest setting. A small orange spark appears in the corner of the screen.

On screen: Rivas (hand on the camera control), Neve (close to the screen), Kofi (spare beacon open on his knees), Lian (at the console, tablet ready)

LIAN And I'd like to read the returns, sir. My eyes are steadier than the automatic shutter.

RIVAS (a beat) She's right.

NEVE She is.

RIVAS All of you right at once. That's a first.

Cormorant, 11:15

Ambience: a faint ticking, the scratch of a knife on plastic

The aft bay is a nest of spare crates. Kofi sits on the floor with a beacon open on his knee and the broken galley timer harnessed to it by tape and wire.

On screen: Kofi (knife in hand, mask half cut on his knee), Neve (crouching, pencil busy), Rivas (leaning in the hatchway)

KOFI Here's the trouble with your drum. It's a pinger. One note, and the note is help. You can't play it like an instrument.

NEVE So make it an instrument.

KOFI I can make it a drum. Cut the drive, run it off this dead galley timer, and the timer makes it beat. Pulse, gap, pulse, and I set the gap on the dial.

RIVAS How loud?

KOFI A thump in the water at a few hundred metres. A knock on a door, not a sonar ping.

NEVE We put the beacon out on a line, away from both boats, toward the wall, and let it beat a long slow rhythm.

NEVE If it comes toward what's warm and what's singing, it comes to the drum. We move the drum. It follows.

KOFI And we walk it away like a dog on a lead.

RIVAS A dog on a lead with a battery that runs down.

RIVAS And if it takes the drum for a threat, and does to the keel what it did to the frame this morning?

NEVE It has never once struck this boat. That's the log, not a theory. Every time we go quiet, it goes quiet. Every time we shout, it comes.

NEVE It answers. And a thing that answers can be given something to answer.

RIVAS It cracked my frame for answering.

NEVE It cracked your frame for shouting. Answering is something else.

LIAN It's like a choir before anyone's told them the tune, sir. Somebody has to give one clear note and hold it.

KOFI And I've got a drum. I'm not saying it's a good soprano. But it's a voice.

RIVAS Doctor. And if it goes wrong and the keel cracks?

NEVE We stop at once, we put every lamp out, and we sit. Silence is the one thing that has always worked.

RIVAS Then it goes in the book. All four of us.

LIAN Sir. The first thing we say should be sorry. Then alive. Then the drum. If they know a noise is coming, they can go flat and hold on.

RIVAS Kofi, cut the mask and finish the drum. Lian, you read the shutter. Doctor, you call the letters. I work the lamp.

The Marrow Deep, 11:48

The spotted beam goes out low across the black and lands on the Halcyon's bent sail as a small bright coin. Below, the crystal crowns lie faint pale blue along the eastern wall.

On screen: Neve (headphones round her neck, tablet in hand), Rivas (hand on the lamp switch), Kofi (at the taped mask, knife in hand), Lian (at the shutter, watching the coin of light)

RIVAS First letter.

NEVE S. Short short short.

LIAN Give her the whole word before she knows it's a word. A signaller hears a rhythm first and reads it second.

NEVE Sorry for silence. All four alive. Cormorant safe. Do you read.

Rivas works the lamp. Then dark for a long minute. Then the little orange eye on the Halcyon's sail wakes.

LIAN (reading from her tablet) Reading you. Halcyon all eleven. Air thirty-three. Who is this.

RIVAS (a short breath out) Tell her.

NEVE Halcyon, this is Doctor Okonkwo aboard Cormorant. There is a living structure in this trench. It answers sound with sound.

NEVE Every ping we send, it answers louder. We went silent and it went quiet. It is now moving toward our boats.

LIAN (after a pause) Why should I trust a scientist with our air.

NEVE Tell her I won't ask her to move or speak or breathe until I have a plan.

RIVAS That's not what I read. That's what you read.

NEVE Log it under my name, then.

RIVAS I'm logging both.

Close-up: a tablet screen with two columns of letters, one in Rivas's blunt capitals and one in Lian's small hand, the second column mostly dots

The left column reads: SORRY. ALIVE. STRUCTURE ANSWERS SOUND. WILL NOT ASK YOU TO MOVE. The right column is a scatter of dots and dashes, waiting to be read.

The Halcyon's lamp blinks out. For a long minute there is nothing. Then it comes back.

LIAN She says: keep lamp on sail. My signaller is reading. But I want to hear a voice.

RIVAS A voice. Tell her no. The phone makes noise.

NEVE I know what the phone makes. But I also know what light is to a tired captain with eleven people. It's a rumour. It's not a person.

RIVAS Then make it as much a person as a lamp can.

NEVE Halcyon. Cormorant has no safe voice. The phone is sound, and sound feeds it. Lamp only.

NEVE I know that is not enough. Trust the light, Captain. I would not ask if I had another way.

The coin blinks it out. The orange eye goes dark for four long minutes.

LIAN She's answering. Reading you. And she asks: what is your plan.

NEVE I have one. I don't have it built yet.

RIVAS Don't tell her that.

NEVE I won't lie to her.

LIAN She's added something. If the structure comes, will you warn us before it does.

NEVE Tell her yes. Two flashes to warn. One to go flat. I will not leave her waiting.

The Halcyon's eye blinks acknowledgement, and nobody aboard the Cormorant speaks.

RIVAS That'll do. Now get me a plan to put under it.
