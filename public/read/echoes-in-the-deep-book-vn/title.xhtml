Echoes in the Deep

Culo

Four kilometres down, a rescue sub finds a stranded crew, a collapsing trench, and something vast that answers every ping with a louder one. With thirty-six hours of air between two hulls, biologist Dr. Neve Okonkwo must convince a divided crew to go dark and listen — because the only way out of the Marrow Deep is to stop shouting and start singing back.
