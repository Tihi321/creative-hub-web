Cormorant, 06:12

Ambience: wind across open water, the slap of waves against two hulls

Grey cloud from edge to edge. The sea runs in long dull ridges. The Cormorant rides low alongside the Halcyon, and the air is so clean it barely registers as a smell.

On screen: Neve (collar up, right hand wrapped in a strip of clean rag), Rivas (cap in his fist, red-eyed), Kofi (ladder hatch open, a plastic crate against his chest)

NEVE Four kilometres of water. The air smells of nothing at all.

NEVE I expected the world to come back rich.

RIVAS It's the Atlantic. It's grey on principle.

KOFI Don't you dare. This is my complaint and I've been saving it.

KOFI This crate has four slivers of the Lattice in it. Three bags, foam, and forty minutes of me in the bilges with a torch in my teeth.

KOFI If one of these breaks I'm going to sit down on this deck and cry, and you're all going to have to watch.

NEVE Put them in the shade.

KOFI Doctor. There is no shade. We are on the sea.

NEVE Then somewhere nobody kicks.

He wedges the crate into a locker between two life jackets and stands over it, opening the lid a finger's width, looking in, closing it again.

Sound: a boat's engine coughing, then settling

RIVAS Halcyon's boat is coming across. Captain's coming herself.

On screen: Captain Achebe (climbing over the rail, worn blue uniform, gold stripes dulled by spray), Rivas (hand out), Neve (waiting behind)

Achebe shakes Rivas's hand, then Neve's, and does not let go of Neve's.

ACHEBE That's a live-contact burn.

NEVE It is.

ACHEBE I've seen a few. You're going to lose the use of part of it. Not all.

ACHEBE It'll stiffen in the cold, and it'll tell you when the weather's coming.

NEVE It can tell me whatever it likes. It saved the boat.

ACHEBE Eleven of mine.

NEVE All eleven. Counting.

ACHEBE I want to say a thing, and I want to say it plainly, because I've had a day and a half to think about it.

ACHEBE When you went quiet down there, I thought you'd left us. I thought you were saving your air and leaving us to drown.

ACHEBE I said so, on my own bridge, and my crew heard me say it.

On screen: Neve (very still), Rivas (looking at the deck), Achebe (letting go of Neve's hand at last)

ACHEBE Then you blinked a lamp at us and apologised. Nobody down there has ever apologised over a lamp before.

ACHEBE I want it in your log that I was wrong and you were right.

NEVE It goes in my log. In all four of our names. And yours, in your own words beside them.

Achebe turns and goes back down to her boat, and the sea takes her without a sound.

RIVAS Twenty minutes of air we had. Most of it in the pipes.

NEVE I'll write the number down. Then I'll write everything else.

Cormorant, 07:02

Ambience: wind across open water

Neve sits on a crate with the log open on her knees and a pen held awkwardly in her good hand. Kofi crouches with the sample crate between his boots.

On screen: Neve (bandaged hand flat on the page to hold it), Kofi (salt in his hair, cut over one eyebrow), Lian (green sonar jacket zipped to the chin), Rivas (leaning on the rail)

KOFI You're holding the pen wrong.

NEVE My other hand's cooked.

KOFI Hold it wrong with feeling, then.

Close-up: the ship's log, open on the crate, three lines in Neve's careful hand

The lines read: 06:20. CAPT. ACHEBE, HALCYON, CROSSED TO CORMORANT. IN HER OWN WORDS AT HER OWN REQUEST, LOGGED IN ALL FOUR NAMES: "I WAS WRONG AND YOU WERE RIGHT." 06:44. ALL ELEVEN OF HALCYON COUNTED. TWENTY MINUTES OF AIR REMAINING ON SURFACING. 06:50. FOUR LATTICE SLIVERS, SAMPLE CRATE, M. MENSAH COLLECTOR.

LIAN Read the last line again.

NEVE Four Lattice slivers, sample crate, Mensah collector.

KOFI Collector. That's a good word for a man who crawled into the bilges while the field settled around him like snow.

KOFI One eyebrow, two hands of jelly, no complaints.

LIAN You complained the whole time.

KOFI I complained about the cold. That's a different complaint.

RIVAS Two hundred and six hours, Wu.

LIAN To the nearest hour.

RIVAS Keep the array in its case. I'm not having some dockhand drop it off a trolley.

LIAN It's sleeping under my bunk until the institute takes it.

The Institute, a debrief room, three weeks later, 10:40

Ambience: rain against tall windows, the chuff of a coffee machine

A long room with tall windows and grey light. A jug of iced water sits on the table and nobody touches it. Twelve biologists along both sides, some with pens.

On screen: Neve (blazer over a plain shirt, bandaged hand in her lap), Rivas (uniform, cap off, hands flat on the table), Lian (tablet and headphones in front of her), Kofi (clean shirt, hair combed badly, sample crate on his knees), a Senior Biologist (glasses on top of her head, pen hovering)

SENIOR BIOLOGIST The company's report is on the table. It is not kind.

SENIOR BIOLOGIST They wrote that the samples could be mineral contamination. That the sonar returns could be a gas vent.

RIVAS The equipment was in a fault. She heard the thing with the equipment broken. That's the point.

SENIOR BIOLOGIST Commander, I'm not trying to—

RIVAS I know what you're trying. Write it this way.

RIVAS Put it down that Commander Rivas, on the record as a man who has never once recommended silence, recommends you take Dr Okonkwo seriously. In my name. All of it.

The room writes. Neve looks at the jug of water and does not touch it.

SENIOR BIOLOGIST Dr Okonkwo. You said the structure learns. Say that properly.

NEVE It remembers a sound. It repeats the sound. It changes the repeat.

NEVE I've watched a great many living things do exactly that. I have never once watched a crystal do it.

SENIOR BIOLOGIST And you're certain the crystal is the organism?

NEVE I'm certain of nothing except what we heard. That's why I brought you the samples.

KOFI That's my cue.

He sets the crate on the bench: four pale grey slivers in bags of cold water. Half the room wears the look of people being polite.

On screen: Lian (standing, headphones in her fist), Neve (watching her), Kofi (stepping back from the bench)

LIAN I have two hundred and six hours of recording. Passive for most of it, so it isn't much to look at.

LIAN But I want you to hear something.

Sound: a single clean ping, then the same ping returning four times louder and bent low by the water

LIAN That's our ping, and that's the answer.

Several pens stop moving.

LIAN Now listen to what it does on the second day.

Sound: a phrase of three hums, then the same phrase again with shorter gaps, then again

On screen: the Senior Biologist (glasses forgotten on top of her head), Lian (steady), Neve (looking at the floor)

LIAN That's not a reflection. That's a reply. It's learning the phrase and leaving less room to answer each time.

SENIOR BIOLOGIST Play the last one. The silence.

Sound: one long low note, gentle and slow, fading

A man at the window makes a sound under his breath that is not a word.

SENIOR BIOLOGIST That is not a rock.

The Institute, a corridor outside the debrief room, 12:15

Ambience: rain against tall windows, a trolley rattling past

Bare corridor. Grey linoleum with a boot-scuff in it. Neve sits on a bench with her bandaged hand between her knees.

On screen: Neve (on the bench, tired), Kofi (crate under one arm, leaning on the wall), Lian (tablet held to her chest), Rivas (hands in his pockets, at the window)

NEVE Eleven days to analyse a recording. I've had grant applications take longer to be looked at.

LIAN They'll say the word in the last session. Organism, without hedging.

KOFI You're shaking, Doctor.

NEVE I'm cold.

KOFI It's a warm building. I've been complaining about it for three hours.

NEVE When they say it, I want it noted. In the report, and on the third line of a piece of paper I'm going to write myself.

RIVAS The third line?

NEVE If you do a thing badly, it's paperwork. If you do it well, it's a fence.

Cormorant, 14:30

Ambience: a shipyard hammering, rain on the hull

The Cormorant is in dock, her forward viewport cut out, the empty frame boarded over. Rain comes in through the open hatch in fine slanting drops.

On screen: Neve (at the chart table, wet hair, pen in her good hand), Kofi (on a crate with a soldering iron and a broken beacon), Rivas (in the hatchway, cap damp)

KOFI Read me that paragraph again.

NEVE No extraction of mineral, biological or acoustic resources from the trench floor or its waters—

KOFI Stop. Three words for the same thing. Use the short one.

NEVE It's a legal document.

KOFI It's a legal document about a rock that sings. Use the short one.

NEVE No taking. Nothing taken from the trench or its water.

KOFI Better. Next.

NEVE Passive buoys on the rim. They listen and do nothing but listen. They do not ping. Ever.

KOFI Then say it that way. A buoy that pings is a loud neighbour with a key.

Close-up: a typed draft page, margins crowded with Kofi's pencil notes, one clause ringed twice

The ringed clause reads: THE SOUNDSCAPE OF THE MARROW DEEP, BEING THE WHOLE ACOUSTIC ENVIRONMENT OF THE TRENCH, IS ITSELF PART OF WHAT IS PROTECTED.

NEVE That one's been trouble. The lawyer wanted it out.

RIVAS The lawyer ever been four kilometres down?

NEVE She asked me why the noise mattered. Not the animal. The noise.

NEVE I told her we're the loudest thing for four kilometres in every direction. We come down into a quiet place and we shout, and then we wonder why it shuts up.

NEVE A quiet place is a kind of heritage. I'd protect it the way I'd protect a church.

RIVAS Put my name under that paragraph.

NEVE It's already there.

RIVAS Good. And it'll be in the yard office as well.

NEVE Sal.

RIVAS Not now. I've got a man in a suit to go and be rude to.

He is gone before she can ask what for. Kofi does not look up from the beacon.

KOFI He's been practising. In the mirror. He told me it wasn't practising.

The Institute, the east pier, autumn, 09:05

Ambience: wind pulling at paper, gulls, a trawler working out of the harbour

Grey sky over a wet pier. The paperwork is under a clear plastic sleeve, held against the wind.

On screen: Lian (hair blowing, reading, mouth slightly open), Neve (coat buttoned, bandaged hand in her pocket), Kofi (scarf, grinning), Rivas (far down the pier, alone)

LIAN It says designated on the recommendation of. It says our names.

NEVE It says your recording. Two hundred and six hours. I only wrote the words.

LIAN You wrote the words.

Lian reads it again. The wind worries the plastic sleeve.

LIAN You know what it is? It's a title. It was just a noise on my screen that I couldn't explain, and I was frightened to say too much about it.

NEVE You said a lot about it. You said it first.

Close-up: the designation schedule under its plastic sleeve, a red square seal bleeding at one corner

The line reads: DESIGNATED ON THE RECOMMENDATION OF DR N. OKONKWO, CMDR S. RIVAS, L. WU, K. MENSAH.

LIAN Read the annex. The bit in Achebe's words.

NEVE The annex records Captain Achebe's correction, at her own request, in her own words. That she was wrong and I was right.

Lian folds the page along its folds, very careful, and puts it inside her jacket.

LIAN I'm keeping that.

NEVE It's a legal document.

LIAN It's our title.

KOFI And my name's on the seal side. Four names, and the seal's bled on the corner. I like it. It looks handmade.

NEVE Go and get the commander. He's been at the end of that pier for an hour pretending to look at a trawler.

A restaurant in Brest, the last night of the crew, 20:40

Ambience: low talk and cutlery, a door to the street opening and shutting

A corner table in a fish restaurant. A candle in a bottle, burned down into its own wax. Four chairs, one empty, a fifth being dragged over.

On screen: Kofi (collar open, ordering with the air of a man awarding medals), Rivas (coat over the chair, cap on the table), Lian (candlelight on her face), Neve (stiff hand resting on the table)

KOFI Fish. For the table. And that one, the flat one, and the little ones in the pan. And all the bread they have.

RIVAS How much bread do they have?

KOFI I'll find out. That's the adventure.

A good gap comes in the conversation late on. Rivas fills it.

RIVAS I've got a thing to say. I'm going to say it once, and nobody at this table is allowed to be kind about it.

RIVAS I was wrong. Not about the air. Not about going quiet. About the other thing.

RIVAS I had a picture in my head of how the world works. You go down, you find the trouble, you get your people out of it.

RIVAS Trouble doesn't have a reason. Trouble is just the thing keeping you from your people.

On screen: Rivas (looking up from his glass), Neve (very still), Kofi (fork down), Lian (watching the candle)

RIVAS Then a woman I'd never met told me the trouble was frightened and wanted to be talked to.

RIVAS And I thought she was a scientist with a soft heart and a hard head, and I'd best manage her carefully until it was over.

RIVAS That's the wrong way round. You weren't managing the deep. You were listening to it. And I was managing you. I'm sorry for it.

Kofi has put down his fork as though it might go off.

RIVAS The log says I was wrong. I want it said where people can hear it, too.

NEVE I have been wrong before. Deeply.

NEVE I called something a threat that wasn't, and a man died, and I've carried that dive ever since like a stone in my pocket.

NEVE I told you about the worm bank because I thought if you knew I could be wrong, you might trust me more.

RIVAS It worked.

NEVE It worked because you were brave enough to be wrong out loud. I wasn't. For years.

NEVE I kept my head down and my name in my own mouth, and never once let anyone see me frightened of being mistaken.

NEVE That's finished. If I'm going to be wrong, I'll do it out loud and get the next one right.

KOFI That'll be a hard habit to keep.

NEVE I know. I've got a burnt hand and a very loud friend to remind me.

KOFI That's me and the hand. To the two of us.

RIVAS To the two of you.

Lian does not lift hers at once. She is looking at the candle.

LIAN I'll miss hearing them talk.

She lifts it. That is that.

The Marrow Deep, 11:20

Ambience: a low quiet hull, water moving past

A small sub descends with its lamps off for the last eight hundred metres. The only light is the soft glow of the instrument panel on two faces.

On screen: Neve (lying at the viewport, bad hand flat on the cold glass), Rosa (bright drysuit, hands calm on the controls)

ROSA You won't hear it at first.

NEVE That's my line.

ROSA I read the report twice. I know all your lines.

NEVE Then I'll try to find you a new one. You'll hear the water. Then you'll hear the water go quiet. Then you'll hear it.

ROSA Depth four thousand. Setting her down soft.

The sediment rises in a slow pale cloud and settles. The lamps are off. The world is soft black, creaking gently with pressure.

ROSA There's the floor. I've got her.

NEVE It's there.

Picture: Neve at the viewport of the quiet spring sub four kilometres down in the Marrow Deep, her hands flat on the glass, while pale luminous crowns spread away into the black beyond, faint at the edges where the light finds them.

ROSA It's exactly where they said. Crowns, spaced out, all along the floor.

NEVE Look at the edges. The light goes into them and doesn't come back the same way.

ROSA They're older than the report. The report's three months old.

NEVE Rocks can be older than their paperwork. Give me the array. Passive.

ROSA Passive is on, Doctor.

Neve puts her mouth close to the pickup and makes one sound. Not quite a note and not quite a word, low and clean and going down, the way a person says something to a horse. It does not carry far.

ROSA Nothing.

NEVE Wait.

Sound: one low note returning, stretched and slow, rising through the hull

It comes back low, stretched by the long cold climb of it, one clean downward curve that settles in Neve's chest.

ROSA (a whisper) That's ours. That's our sound.

NEVE It's different and it's the same. The cold bends it down on the way back.

ROSA What do I do?

NEVE Nothing. We sit still and let it finish.

The sound fades. The field settles. The dark goes back to being the dark.

NEVE That's the neighbours. They know us now.

ROSA That's a strange thing to say at four kilometres.

NEVE It's a strange four kilometres.

ROSA Doctor. If I wanted to do this every spring. Would they let me?

NEVE Passive only, no sonar, a slow descent, and a permit with a name on it. You'd have to be quiet and patient.

ROSA I can be quiet. I've been quiet since we launched.

NEVE Then I'll ask for a second name on the third line.

Neve lies at the window with her crooked hand open on the glass, and does not call it a threat, and does not call it an enemy, and does not call it anything but what it is.
