The Lighthouse That Kept Lists

Tobias Wren

When a decommissioned lighthouse on a small Dalmatian island begins printing lists in the night, sceptical radio engineer Mara Bašić has one week to find out who is writing them before the harbour master scraps the old equipment for good. Bread, lemons, an apology to the woman who runs the shop — the lists know things only her late grandfather could have known. A warm, funny story about the people who love us, the instructions they leave behind, and the ones that make us laugh out loud.
