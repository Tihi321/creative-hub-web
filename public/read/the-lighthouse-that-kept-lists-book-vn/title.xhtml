The Lighthouse That Kept Lists

When the dead lighthouse on a Dalmatian island begins printing shopping lists at ten o'clock at night, sceptical radio engineer Mara Bašić has four nights to find the transmitter before the harbour master signs the old station away. Between the bora, the lemons and a teleprinter that types in her grandfather's capitals, she must decide what is worth keeping and what she has been explaining away for years.
