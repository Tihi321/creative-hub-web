The Storm Chaser's Secret

Sixteen-year-old Wren Kalisz built her own storm rig to prove lightning can be fired early — but when the once-a-decade supercell finally comes, her instruments read something impossible: the storm is being steered, and it is headed straight for her town. To stop a crew that believes it is saving lives, Wren has to put her machine, her theory and herself inside the one place no chaser survives by luck.
