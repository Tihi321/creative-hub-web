Echoes in the Deep

Mara Quill

Four kilometres down, a rescue sub finds a stranded crew, a collapsing trench, and something on the sea floor that answers every sound with a louder one. With the hull cracking and the air running out, biologist Neve Okonkwo must convince a skeptical commander to do the one thing no rescue has ever dared: go silent, and listen.
