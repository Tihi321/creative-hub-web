When the half-life of carbon-14 shortens by a fraction of a percent, three specialists aboard a generations-old arkship must decide what a changing universe means: a message from a creator, an environment to adapt to, or a crowd to keep calm. As the ship's physics drifts toward a breaking point, each of them builds the only thing they can — a fix small enough to save the people beside them.
