The Snail and the Salad

Culo

Tino is a very small snail with a very big appetite, and the best lettuce in the world is waiting on the far side of the garden. One tiny step at a time, past pebbles like mountains and sprinklers that go fsss, he sets off to find it. Slow is still moving, Mama always says, and Tino has a crunchy surprise waiting at the end.
