The Snail and the Salad

Under the big green leaf, morning. Dew shines on the huge green leaf, and Tino peeps out of his round caramel-brown shell with a sleepy smile.

On screen: Tino (round caramel shell, pale green body, sleepy smile).

Tino wakes up in his little round shell.

His tummy goes rumble, rumble.

He is very, very hungry.

The flowerpot, morning. Mama points one antenna past the terracotta pot while Tino listens with both dot eyes wide.

On screen: Mama (deep brown shell, tiny pink flower, kind eyes), Tino (pale green body, listening).

Mama says the salad is on the other side of the garden.

She says slow is still moving.

Tino nods and smiles.

The pebble path, morning. Tino sets off along the grey pebbles, his little foot going tap, tap, tap on the stones.

On screen: Tino (tiny, cheerful, shell on his back).

Tino sets off along the pebble path.

Slowly, slowly, slowly, one tiny step after another.

The garden is very, very big.

The pebble path, midday. One big pebble towers over Tino like a mountain, and he climbs it with a happy grin.

On screen: Tino (halfway up, brave, grinning).

One pebble is as big as a mountain.

Tino climbs up, up, up.

Then he slides down, wheee!

The spotted mushroom, midday. The sprinkler sprays a silver shower and Tino hides under the red spotted cap.

On screen: Tino (peeping out, shell wet), the shiny sprinkler.

The sprinkler goes fsss!

Drip, drop, drip.

Tino waits under the spotted mushroom until the shower stops.

The tomato row, afternoon. Buba lands beside Tino on a big green leaf, her red wings closing with a flit.

On screen: Tino (looking up), Buba (red with seven black spots, wings closing).

Buba lands beside him with a flit.

She says she will fly ahead and wait.

Off she goes, loop the loop!

The tomato row, afternoon. A gardener's huge green boot stomps into the dark soil beside the tomato canes.

On screen: Tino (pulled into his shell), a big green boot, three red tomatoes.

A big boot stomps down.

Thump!

Tino pulls into his shell, and the boot stomps past.

The lettuce patch, afternoon. Buba waits on a wide crisp leaf, and rows of bright green lettuce shine in the sun.

On screen: Buba (seven black spots, happy), rows of crinkly lettuce.

Buba waits on a big crisp leaf.

The whole patch is green and crunchy.

Tino's tummy rumbles again.

The lettuce patch, late afternoon. Tino takes one tiny bite of a fat lettuce leaf, his eyes closed with delight.

On screen: Tino (tiny bite, eyes closed, very happy).

Tino takes one tiny bite.

CRUNCH!

It is the best salad in the whole wide garden.

The lettuce patch, late afternoon. A little round hole goes right through the leaf, and Tino sits beside it, very full.

On screen: Tino (round belly, sleepy smile), the round hole.

He eats a little round hole right through the leaf.

Munch, munch, munch.

Tino is very, very full.

The pebble path, evening. Tino carries a small piece of lettuce on his shell as the garden turns golden.

On screen: Tino (leaf on shell, slow steps), long golden shadows.

Tino carries a small piece of lettuce home for Mama.

Slowly, slowly, slowly.

The sun sinks low and the garden turns gold.

The flowerpot, night. Mama and Tino share the leaf under the terracotta pot, and the silver moon shines over them.

On screen: Mama (deep brown shell, tiny pink flower), Tino (curled in his shell, yawning).

Mama and Tino share the leaf.

Tino yawns and curls up in his shell.

Goodnight, little snail.
