The Silent Signal

Culo

Salvage-tug engineer Mara Kell is hired to silence a faulty beacon on a storm-battered island so the insurers will reopen a closed shipping lane. But the pulse isn't a fault — it's a signal keeping time with the tide, and it carries her drowned brother's last words. To hear the whole of it, Mara must trust a secretive keeper's son and outrun the contractor who paid her to keep the island quiet.
