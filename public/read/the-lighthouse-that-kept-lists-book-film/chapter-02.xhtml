The Back Room

The harbour, night

Shot (wide, tracking, 6s): Dino marches along the riva holding up a fishbone antenna made of tape measures; Mara walks beside him with a chirping receiver.

On screen: Dino (navy jacket, cap, solemn), Mara (yellow raincoat, hood up, focused)

Sound: wind, two warbling radio tones growing louder

MARA (focused) Left. Slowly. Stop. There.

DINO (solemn) For the record, I feel ridiculous.

Ivka's back room, night

Shot (medium, slow push-in, 8s): Ivka sits at the card table with coffee, her hand on a silver cassette recorder wired to a black radio; she looks up without surprise.

On screen: Ivka (black cardigan, grey knitted hat, glasses on a chain, unbothered)

Sound: two tones squabbling from a small speaker, heater ticking

IVKA (unbothered) Shut the door. You're letting the bora in.

Shot (medium, static, 8s): Ivka lays her hand on a shoebox of four cassettes; Mara and Dino stand dripping by the door.

On screen: Ivka (calm, a little proud), Mara (soaked, stunned), Dino (holding the antenna, embarrassed)

IVKA One a night, when Mara comes home. He said don't tell her. She'll explain it.

Shot (close-up, slow push-in, 10s): Ivka holds a strip of teleprinter paper at arm's length; her mouth tightens, then softens.

On screen: Ivka (reading, moved, hiding it)

ANTE (V.O.) For Ivka. Nineteen seventy-one. I did not come to the dance. I dance like a winch. Sorry.

IVKA (dry) He did dance like a winch.

The service room, night

Music: slow solo piano with a soft mandolin, tender, warm

Shot (medium, slow push-in, 6s): Mara reads the paper climbing from the teleprinter; behind her Ivka sits on the crate in a huge orange life jacket, Dino by the door.

On screen: Mara (yellow raincoat, reading), Ivka (orange life jacket over her black cardigan), Dino (clipboard to his chest)

Sound: teleprinter keys

ANTE (V.O.) For Mara. Do not sell the house.

Shot (close-up, static, 6s): Mara's face in the red and green lamplight; she swallows.

On screen: Mara (yellow raincoat, holding back tears)

ANTE (V.O.) You can stop explaining things now. I know how it works. I built it.

Shot (two-shot, static, 6s): Mara turns, explaining with her hands; Dino holds out an ironed handkerchief and she takes it, crying.

On screen: Mara (crying, still talking), Dino (gentle, handkerchief held out)

MARA (shaky) The tape plays into the radio, so it's actually...

DINO (gently) Mara.

The harbour, dusk

Shot (medium, static, 6s): At the ferry ramp a workman in an orange jacket signs the heritage form on Dino's clipboard as the lighthouse lamp comes on behind them.

On screen: Dino (clipboard, quietly triumphant)

DINO (formal) The lighthouse is a cultural good now. Nobody touches it.

The service room, evening

Shot (close-up, slow push-in, 8s): Mara writes in a new black notebook, a loaf and two lemons beside it.

On screen: Mara (yellow raincoat, calm, a small smile)

MARA (V.O.) Light on. Wind dropping. Teleprinter OK. Bread. Two lemons.

Music: stop
