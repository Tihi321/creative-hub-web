The Lighthouse That Kept Lists

Mara Quill

When sceptical radio engineer Mara returns to her grandfather's windswept island to empty his house, a teleprinter in the lighthouse starts printing lists again, one a night, in the voice of a man dead since spring. Bread, lemons, an apology to the shopkeeper, and details only he could know. To find who is sending them, she will need a harbour master with a clipboard, a tape-measure antenna, and the courage to stop explaining everything away.
