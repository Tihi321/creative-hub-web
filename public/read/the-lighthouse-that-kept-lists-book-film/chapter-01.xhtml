Bread, Lemons

The harbour, late afternoon

The bora combs the grey sea into white stripes. The white lighthouse at the end of its causeway is not yet lit.

Title: The Lighthouse That Kept Lists

Music: solo mandolin and soft accordion, wistful and light, slow

Shot (extreme wide, slow push-in, 6s): A small white ferry ploughs through wind-streaked water toward a low grey island; at the end of a long stone causeway stands a white lighthouse on a rock.

Sound: wind, ferry engine, waves slapping the hull

Shot (medium, handheld, 4s): On the open deck Mara rolls the cuffs of a yellow raincoat two sizes too big; the wind unrolls them at once.

On screen: Mara (oversized yellow raincoat, short dark hair blown flat, tired)

Sound: wind

Ante's house, morning

Grey light on the green front door; lists in capitals pinned on the kitchen walls behind.

Shot (two-shot, static, 6s): Dino fills the doorway, clipboard pressed to his chest, and holds up a strip of yellowed teleprinter paper to Mara.

On screen: Dino (navy harbour-master jacket, peaked cap, official), Mara (yellow raincoat over a sweater, sleepy)

DINO (official) The lighthouse printed this. Bread. Lemons. One apology to Ivka.

Shot (two-shot, slow push-in, 6s): Mara hands the strip back with a shrug; Dino glances up the empty street, where a plastic bag tumbles past.

On screen: Mara (dry), Dino (unimpressed)

MARA (dry) A prank. Kids.

DINO (flat) The youngest person on this island is forty-one.

Ivka's shop, day

Warm bulb light, dried figs on a cord behind the old till, a crate of lemons on the counter.

Shot (medium, slow push-in, 10s): Behind the counter Ivka folds two lemons into a paper bag and looks at Mara over her glasses.

On screen: Ivka (black cardigan, glasses on a chain, grey knitted hat, knowing), Mara (yellow raincoat, a loaf under her arm)

IVKA (matter-of-fact) Two lemons every Monday. Fifteen years after Katica died.

IVKA (dry) I said, she's not drinking tea. He said, you don't know that.

Ante's house, evening

Shot (medium, slow push-in, 4s): At the oilcloth kitchen table Mara sets the bag of lemons beside her laptop and a radio dongle, looks at a photo of the paper strip on her phone, and stands up.

On screen: Mara (yellow raincoat, oil on her fingers, decided)

MARA (to herself) Somebody's transmitting.

The service room, night

One bare bulb; the lighthouse beam flashes past the small window.

Shot (medium, static, 6s): Mara sits on an upturned crate beside the grey teleprinter; the red lamp flickers, then the green, and the machine crashes awake. She jumps up.

On screen: Mara (yellow raincoat, startled)

Sound: teleprinter clatter, wind

Shot (close-up, slow push-in, 8s): Yellowed paper climbs out of the teleprinter line by line, the red and green lamps glowing side by side.

Sound: teleprinter keys hammering

ANTE (V.O.) Boats that did not come back. Galeb. Sveti Nikola, nineteen eighty-three.

Shot (close-up, static, 6s): Mara's face in the bulb light as the beam sweeps past the window behind her; her eyes fill.

On screen: Mara (yellow raincoat, shaken, eyes wet)

ANTE (V.O.) Marica. Went to Italy.

MARA (whispering) Nobody knew that.

Music: stop
