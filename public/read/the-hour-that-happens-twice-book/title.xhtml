The Hour That Happens Twice

Culo

When a handless clock face marked HUIT leads an Irish locksmith and a French-Hungarian restorer from a foggy grave in Père-Lachaise to Prague in the week the clocks go back, they find a speaking clock who has lost a Thursday and a brotherhood that means to rob thousands of pensioners in the hour that happens twice. To stop them, Dec and Inès must open a lock that only a voice can sing, before the second two o'clock strikes. Laughter, a chill, and partners at last.
