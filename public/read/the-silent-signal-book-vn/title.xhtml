The Silent Signal

Culo

A salvage-tug engineer is hired to silence a faulty beacon on a storm-bound island, only to find the signal is a message her drowned brother left behind twelve years ago — and that someone has been paying to keep it quiet. To bring the truth to the surface she will have to dive a wreck at the edge of safety, outrun a contractor with everything to bury, and broadcast one dead man's voice through the storm.
