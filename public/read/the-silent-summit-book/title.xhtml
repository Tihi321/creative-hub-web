The Silent Summit

Culo

A deaf mountaineer leads a doubting team up an unnamed 7,000-metre peak to recover a lost bronze bell, reading the mountain by vibration and sight — and when a whiteout strands them above a shattered icefall, she must use those same senses to bring them all down alive. A story of hard-won awe, about how listening is not the same as hearing, and how no one survives a mountain alone.
