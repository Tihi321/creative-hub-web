Windward Drift

Culo

Sailmaker Nella Farrow leaves the last harbor with sixty days of water and a chart drawn by a famous soloist, aiming to repeat his celebrated crossing of a compass-broken ocean. Midway across, she hauls a drifting buoy aboard and finds a log that tells a different story — and the water itself tells her the route she inherited no longer exists. To reach the far atoll, she must navigate by wind and stars on a course no one has ever charted.
