The Salt Line

Tobias Wren

Broke and four payments from losing her father's salvage tug, Juno Halloway takes one last job: hauling a grounded freighter off a reef before the autumn surge poisons her town's only fresh water. But the freighter's holds are empty, her crew is hiding, and her captain ran the ship aground on purpose. Now Juno has forty hours to decide what salvage really means — and who pays for it.
