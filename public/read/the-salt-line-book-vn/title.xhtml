The Salt Line

Culo

Broke, stubborn salvage captain Juno Halloway has forty hours to haul a grounded freighter off a hidden reef before the autumn surge poisons her town's only freshwater. But the freighter's master ran her aground on purpose, and what waits in her sealed lower hold is worth more than any salvage award — and far more dangerous. Salt-bitten and wind-scoured, this is a story about the hard right thing, and the town that remembers it.
