The Tow

Meridian Dawn, day

Ambience: gulls, water slapping a hull in the flats

Two ships in the estuary mouth and nothing else. Pell's launch is a white speck sliding in past the bar. The freighter sits half on the mud, half in the flood, heeled a few degrees to starboard.

On screen: Juno (at the tug's wheel, coat collar up), Ines Voss (aboard the Dawn's bridge, small across the water)

Juno stays at the wheel. She watches the tide climb the Dawn's side and take her weight.

Meridian Dawn, afternoon

Ambience: a line running through hands, a shackle knocking steel

The tug lies alongside the freighter's bow. Canvas and chain are laid out across the Ninth Hour's deck in a long gray fold.

On screen: Teo (kneeling, hands on the canvas), Juno (above him), Barrow (coiling line), Fen (taking a turn at the rail)

TEO This is all of her. The mat, the chains, the softner against the iron. She was built to hold.

JUNO And the seam wants holding before the next flood.

TEO So we make her still and draw it down. An hour's work if the sea leaves us be.

Fen pays the messenger out and Barrow hauls it under the forefoot, ducking his head beneath the spring of the hull, and it comes up dripping on the starboard side.

FEN Messenger's round.

TEO Then bend her on. Chain along the bottom edge, or she billows and goes.

Meridian Dawn, afternoon

Ambience: a chart rustling, a screw turning slow

The wheelhouse is dim. Voss stands over the chart with her finger on a long finger of sand.

On screen: Ines Voss (face gray, finger steady), Juno (hands on the table)

VOSS The west bar. Water over it at the top of the tide and no more. The mud is soft and it has never been dug. She will go in to her bilge and lie quiet.

JUNO You'll take her yourself.

VOSS There is no hiding in it now. You will take the tug on her bow. I will not get her round the bar on my own engine.

JUNO Then we take her down while the water's on the flats. Not a touch more than we can help.

VOSS Not a touch.

Kestrel Reach, afternoon

Ambience: a diesel under load, mud taking a hull

The flats run out gray and green to meet the freighter. She comes down slow, touches, and goes on, and lies down in the mud the way a tired person lies down.

On screen: Juno (at the tug's wheel, throttle hand still), Teo (at the rail with the mat crew), Ines Voss (at the Dawn's wheel)

Sound: a long soft grinding, then a settling

TEO She's touching. Ease the strain. Let her walk in on her own way.

JUNO Taking the line easy.

The bow comes up, the stern goes down, the mud holds her to the bilge. Barrow and Fen haul the messenger and the mat comes down over the starboard seam and lies flat.

TEO Mat's down. Chains are holding.

JUNO How long?

TEO The next flood tells us.

Meridian Dawn, night

Ambience: a pump hammering, water leaving a forepeak

The night is long and it goes by in the sound of water. The pump's suction sits in the forepeak, its discharge led aft into the deep tanks. The ship sits down by the stern to the tune of it.

On screen: Juno (on the tug's quarter, watching gauges), Barrow (walking the rail with a lantern), Davy (at the lower hold hatch), Fen (in the wheelhouse door)

DAVY Lower hold's dry. Bilge hasn't moved since dark.

FEN Radio's quiet. Town's boats are at the mouth and staying.

JUNO Tell them to keep their lights low. Barrow, dip yours once and they'll answer.

Barrow lifts the lantern along the rail. Out on the flats the lanterns dip and come back up, low and steady.

Meridian Dawn, dawn

Ambience: a pump ticking over, a slow water going out

Gray light. The surge has gone out down the channel. The flats stand out of the sea and the freighter lies in the mud with the mat on her side like a bandage.

On screen: Teo (mud to the waist, flat-voiced), Juno (coming up the deck)

TEO Tanks took all they'd hold and the discharge went over the side in the night. Forepeak water, out of the channel and back into the channel. Sumps are dry and they'll be dry at morning.

JUNO Have it in your log what it was.

TEO It's in the log.

He sits down on the hatch coaming and lets his head hang, and the wind comes across the flats and smells of rain and nothing else.

Kestrel Reach, morning

Ambience: gulls on wet sand, a tide going out

Juno walks the north lip of the estuary mouth alone, out where the water goes over into the flats and down through the sand into the lens. The flats are wet and gray and the gulls stand on them.

On screen: Juno (alone, coat to the wind, looking at the sand)

Picture: Juno on the wet gray flats at Kestrel Reach at dawn, a low brown salt mark curving from the channel toward the north well and stopping short of it, the beached Meridian Dawn behind her with the mat on her side

She crouches and puts her hand flat on the sand at the end of the brown line.

JUNO There. You come to the well and you stop.

She walks the line twice, both ways, and stands at the well and looks back at the channel.

Meridian Dawn, morning

Ambience: wind over the flats, a pump ticking over

The deck is canted and drying underfoot. Teo sits with his hands black to the elbow. Barrow stands at the rail.

On screen: Juno (coming over the side, log under her arm), Teo (raising his head), Barrow (turning)

JUNO The low water came to the well and stopped. North, south, and the one by the school are behind that line.

TEO You're sure.

JUNO I put my hand on the sand at the end of it. It's in the log with the hour.

BARROW Then it held.

JUNO It held. One of your pumps did all the work of it.

Barrow looks at Teo and then at the flats, and says nothing, and the nothing is a different sort of silence from the ones he used before.

Ninth Hour, morning

Ambience: a hand pump working, a slow swell under the hull

The old tug lies on the Dawn's quarter, fenders crushed flat, paint scored, stack black, a list to port where the freighter came against her. Teo climbs to the wheelhouse with a smear of oil on his forehead.

On screen: Juno (log under her arm), Teo (spanner in hand)

TEO We'll want hands in the morning, to take the drums off her in the light.

JUNO Pell's got the court for that and the court's got the men in the paper suits. We'll want hands to pump her and watch her and keep the mat down till they come. And we'll want the town.

TEO That's a thing you're good at. Asking the town.

JUNO It's a thing that's been done to me. There's a difference. I'm learning it.

TEO And the boat? She'll want her slip and her parts and the sum's the same sum it was on Monday.

JUNO Then we'll do it the way we do everything else.

TEO Badly and last.

JUNO Badly and last.

He laughs with no joy in it and some warmth, and goes back down the ladder.

Meridian Dawn, dusk

Ambience: a tide making over the flats, wind to the northeast

The wind has gone round and the tide is coming back gray and slow over the flats. The freighter lies in the middle of them with the drums dry in her and the mark down at the well.

On screen: Juno (coat collar up, log in her lap), Barrow (standing beside her with a mug)

Close-up: the log's ruled page, a hard hand filling it line by line, the mayday, the reef, the empty holds, the drums, the mat and the tow and the going onto the flats

She writes it down from the beginning. It takes her four pages and the afternoon.

BARROW Tea. It's not good tea.

JUNO It never is.

BARROW I watched the whole of it. I said a thing slow that wanted saying, and it was wrong to say it.

JUNO You'll have a court to say it to, in the proper way.

BARROW Aye. I will.

He stands with her while she finishes and says nothing more.

Meridian Dawn, dusk

Ambience: a motor launch coming in slow, water slapping a hull

The launch comes across the gray flats with Pell in the bow and a paper in his hand.

On screen: Teo (coming up the hatch, spanner in hand), Juno (closing the log, standing)

TEO Pell's coming out. He's got a paper and he's reading it and shaking his head at it.

JUNO Then it's the court, and the court won't pay.

TEO It never does. Come and hear it plainly, and then come below and sleep in your wet coat like the rest of us.

Juno stands and looks out at the beached freighter and the mark on the sand and the gray clean water beyond the salt line.

The town's wells stand behind the mark. The drums stand dry above a dry bilge. Every light in Kestrel Reach is coming on, one by one, all up the hill.

JUNO Bring him alongside. I'll hear what the town has to say.
