Halyard's Man

Meridian Dawn, late afternoon

Ambience: a slow iron grind on rock, water tipping under the floor plates

The wheelhouse lamp throws a weak yellow arc down the ladder shaft. Below it the lower hold is black steel and cold air.

On screen: Juno (halfway down the ladder, torch in her teeth, one hand on the rail), Teo (below, crouched over the crate, gloves on)

JUNO How long since the last one?

TEO Twenty minutes. The bead drops a finger's width, swells up, and I look away and back, and it's started again.

JUNO Then it's seeping. Not weeping. Seeping.

TEO Word for a word, Juno. Neither one floats.

JUNO Show me the folder.

He wipes his gloves on his thigh and lays the papers flat on the crate lid and turns them to the light.

TEO Deck log. Fair hand, the mate's. Last whole page before the torn ones. Pilot declined at the seaward mark. Owner's agent advised the master by radio to proceed without pilotage.

Close-up: a ruled page in a fair clerk's hand, the agent's advice entered, and beneath it, signed, four words in a rougher script: I will not.

Juno reads it twice. She puts one finger on the four words and does not lift it.

JUNO She was told to run her without a pilot. She wrote down that she wouldn't.

TEO And then she ran her west into a strait in the dark.

JUNO Yes.

TEO So nobody ordered her onto that rock.

JUNO No. She chose it.

Sound: a deep engine note, low and steady, climbing through the plates

Teo's head comes up. The jar in his hand tips and he steadies it.

TEO That's not the Ninth Hour's beat.

JUNO No.

TEO That's a big animal. Hundred ton of animal.

JUNO That's Rusk.

TEO In this fog he'll be on the reef with us.

JUNO He won't. He's got the sounder and the old chart, and he's had them longer than I have.

JUNO Bar the panel. Crate in the paint locker. Not one word to the three of them, not while that man is in the water.

TEO And you?

JUNO I'll go and be polite to him.

Meridian Dawn, late afternoon

Ambience: white water working the reef, a heavy diesel throttling down

Fog lies on the water in gray wool. A deck light swings once across the gray and steadies on the freighter's flank. The big tug comes out of it behind the light, wide and high, her paint a hard yellow, her name across her bow in foot-tall letters.

On screen: Juno (at the rail, oilskin streaked with rust), Teo (at her shoulder, torch still warm in his fist), Doran Rusk (boots apart, hands on hips, yellow oilskin, grinning)

RUSK Ahoy the freighter! Ahoy the Ninth Hour! Juno Halloway, is that you behind that tub?

JUNO Afternoon, Rusk. You're a long way off your patch.

RUSK Same water as anybody.

He looks her boat up and down, then the freighter, and puts his head back and laughs, a big warm roll of it.

RUSK Barely abeam of the reef, a freighter full of nothing, and a crew of three. Here's a fine thing. Halyard's been on the phone since the small hours. They want her off the reef and the sea out of her. So they called me.

He taps his chest.

RUSK I've got the contract. I've got six-inch wire and a winch like a church organ. What have you got?

JUNO A claim lodged and first light on the vessel.

RUSK First light. You know what first light's worth? Nothing in the morning and less by afternoon. What's worth anything is the award, and the court listens to the man who saved the most ship. Not the woman who sat on it.

RUSK Here's what I'll do. Stand down. Take your line off her and take your boat back up the harbor. I'll write you a cheque for two thousand for your trouble and put you down as assisting salvor.

JUNO Two thousand.

RUSK Token. I said it was token. It's a token for a token claim.

RUSK What have you got, Juno? A hull with nothing in it and a crew playing dumb. If I can't move her by nightfall I'll eat my hat. And if I can't move her at all, I'll report your claim as interference and let the court sort out who was in the right of it.

He puts a boot on the rail and rests his elbow on his knee.

RUSK You know what a fine does to a woman who hasn't made her last four payments? It takes the boat. I hear things, captain. Harbor's a small place. You're four behind, aren't you. Eleven thousand and change.

The cold comes up through Juno's boots. She keeps her face the way she keeps it when a tow rope parts.

On screen: Teo (very still, jaw tight)

JUNO You've done your homework.

RUSK I've done my paperwork. There's a difference.

Meridian Dawn, late afternoon

Ambience: white water working the reef, a heavy diesel idling

They meet on the freighter's afterdeck, Rusk up his own rope ladder, the fog swirling between the structures and the reef grumbling under the plates.

On screen: Doran Rusk (close now, hands loose at his sides), Juno (three paces off), Teo (at the deckhouse corner, arms folded)

RUSK Here's the way of it. You're going to tell me about the abandoned-vessel rule. And I'm going to tell you that ship had a master aboard when you boarded her.

JUNO The crew hid her. Three of them stood on that deck and lied to me. I boarded in the belief she was abandoned and I lodged my claim on it.

RUSK It stands until it falls. No master present, you told Pell. Then your three sailors let slip she's aboard, and the harbor log will say so, and I'll have my lawyer pull it. A salvor who boards a crewed ship needs the master's say. I don't hear a master saying anything.

JUNO Then find her. Ask her crew. They won't say.

RUSK Then she's not a master. She's a stowaway.

He laughs again and rubs his hands.

RUSK Here's the truth, and I'll say it plain because I like you and always have. You've got a tug nine years past her pension and an engineer who fixes it with crab wire. I've got a winch that'll pull a lighthouse off its rock. Take the two thousand. Sign the assistance paper. Go home.

JUNO I'll sign nothing that puts your hook on this ship.

RUSK Then it's the court.

JUNO It's the court.

Rusk looks at her. His grin goes thin and stays.

RUSK Nightfall. If that tub's still on that reef at dark, I move her, and I move her the way I like, and I put the whole business in front of a judge. You'll be lucky to keep your license, never mind your boat.

He turns for the ladder, then stops and looks back.

RUSK And Juno. Whatever's down her holds that's got these people lying, you don't want to be standing next to it when it comes up.

On screen: Doran Rusk (over the rail, down the ladder, gone into the fog)

The Gannet's engine notes up. The yellow hull slides backward and steadies off the stern, and the sound of her winch turning over begins.

TEO He's not leaving.

JUNO No. He's parking.

Meridian Dawn, late afternoon

Ambience: a winch turning over, slow and patient

Teo has gone below with the sample. The fog lies along the deckhouse and fills the space between the structures, and a shape stands in it: a length of jaw under a cap pulled low, a heavy gray coat, a ship's apron. Nothing else shows.

On screen: Ines Voss (a gray shape in the fog, still as a rock), Juno (turned, fists at her sides)

VOSS Captain Halloway.

JUNO You're her.

VOSS I'm aboard.

JUNO Then step out here and say it to me properly.

VOSS Not yet. Not with that man's winch turning where he can hear me. You've a claim and a court and a town up the estuary, and I'll not have my name said in the open before it's ready to be said in a courtroom.

JUNO He'll have your name off the harbor log by supper.

VOSS Yes. He will.

JUNO And then he'll put his line on your stern and take you out past the shelf and scuttle you, and your thinking will have a short run.

VOSS Yes. It will.

JUNO I've been down your lower hold. I've seen the drums and I've smelled them and I've had a jar sealed off the one that's leaking.

The shape does not move. But something in the fog changes, the way air changes before rain.

VOSS Then you know what I am.

JUNO I know what's in your hold. I don't know what you are.

VOSS You've read my log.

JUNO I've read four words in your log. I will not: that's what she wrote. That's the whole of what I know, and it's not enough to haul a ship off a rock with.

VOSS It's more than I've given any man in two years.

She draws a breath, and the breath is not steady, and she makes it steady.

VOSS You want to know what your line will do before you put it on. So hear it. She's whole at the forefoot. She's taken water forward of her collision bulkhead, and the bulkhead holds her still, and the bulkhead will fail before the bottom does.

JUNO Then I run two lines and take the strain slow.

VOSS Slow and fore and aft both, or you'll tear her shoulder open on the rock and put what's in her into the strait. And then the estuary. And then the wells. That's your seam and that's your ship, and it's all the answer I've got in me.

JUNO Why this reef? Why a channel with a town on the end of it?

VOSS Because a narrow strait makes the news and a dock wall doesn't.

JUNO That's not an answer. That's a riddle with a coat on.

VOSS It's the answer I have.

JUNO Then we'll get you your courtroom.

Voss looks at her. For a moment the fog thins and there is a face in it: narrow, weathered, deep-set eyes, and the fear in it is held absolutely still.

VOSS The water's coming over that reef at dark. Mind what I told you, and mind it twice.

Sound: a boot on wet steel, going for'ard

The fog takes her. Juno goes after three steps and stops. The foredeck is empty but for the windlass, and the chain-locker hatch stands open and dark.

Meridian Dawn, dusk

Ambience: water working the hull, a winch turning over

Juno comes up out of the chain-locker hatch and bars it behind her with the pin. Teo waits at the break of the foredeck with his jar wrapped in a rag. The fog lies on the water and the reef shows white and hides.

On screen: Teo (jar in both hands, soot on his cheek), Juno (wiping her palms on her thighs)

TEO Well?

JUNO She gave me the bulkhead and she gave me the wells and she gave me nothing with a name on it.

TEO She talked to you, though.

JUNO She talked. She's not a fool and she's not a coward, and those two things together on a grounded ship with drums in her scare me more than Rusk does.

TEO Rusk's got a winch.

JUNO Rusk wants to be paid. She wants something else, and I don't know its shape yet.

Sound: a long rifle-shot crack running out along the starboard rail

The plates move under her soles and the jar in Teo's hands tips and he catches it against his chest.

TEO There it is. She's working.

JUNO How long has she got?

TEO Sound hull, whole bulkheads, two lines and a deal of patience and she comes off. Except she's pinned on that shoulder, and the swell's filing her like a nail, and every turn moves her an inch she doesn't want to move.

JUNO Hours?

TEO Before the surge, four. Before she starts hurting herself, less.

TEO Juno. When she opens, sixty drums go into the Bellmouth, and the Bellmouth runs up my estuary, and the town drinks out of a lens under the flats. That's not a fine and it's not a license. That's a town with no water.

JUNO I know what it is.

TEO If Rusk tows her out past the shelf, what's in her goes into three hundred fathoms and stops being anybody's trouble. And nobody ever proves a thing.

JUNO And if I tow her into the estuary and beach her on the flats, a court walks her decks in daylight.

TEO If she goes over the bar. The bar's shallow and the south channel's narrow and I don't like her bottom.

JUNO Then we don't touch bottom. Tell me what you need.

TEO Two lines, fore and aft, both on the winch. The collision mat over that shoulder before we take the strain, and I've got one pair of hands for it and that's mine.

JUNO There's three pair standing in the forepeak wishing they were somewhere else.

TEO They won't work for you. The bosun won't, a man who's lawyered himself quiet before he's been charged with anything.

JUNO He will. He's a bosun and his ship's under him, and a bosun's a bosun before he's a witness. I'll put it to him as work, not as a question.

Picture: Juno and Teo at the Meridian Dawn's break of the foredeck at dusk, the yellow Iron Gannet hove to off the stern with her winch turning, a white line of reef beyond the bow, the sky a flat gray ceiling

TEO And if the master won't give you her bottom?

JUNO She's given me her bulkhead and her wells and her seam. I'll take those three things and put them in a court's lap and let a judge ask her for the rest.

TEO That's a long row to hoe in four hours.

JUNO Then we start hoeing.

Meridian Dawn, dusk

Ambience: a winch turning over, wind along the rail

The three crew stand bunched at the forepeak rail with the reef behind them showing white and hiding. Barrow has his arms folded. Fen's collar is up. Davy's rag is wound so tight around his knuckles the blood is out of his fingers.

On screen: Barrow (weathered face wet with spray), Fen (eyes on the reef), Davy (winding the rag), Juno (approaching)

JUNO Bosun. I'm not here to ask you a question.

BARROW That'll be a change.

JUNO I'm here to tell you the ship's going to come off this reef and go south into the estuary before the surge, and I've one pair of hands for the mat and I need three.

BARROW You'll beach her on the flats.

JUNO I will.

BARROW With what's in her.

JUNO With what's in her, in the light, where a court can see it. Or I can leave her here and let the swell file her open and let what's in her go up the estuary and into the wells of Kestrel Reach. Those are the two. You can work the mat or you can stand here and watch the second one happen.

Barrow looks at the reef, and at the Gannet's winch turning over the water.

FEN Barrow.

BARROW I heard her.

He unfolds his arms.

BARROW Fen, the mat's in the forepeak locker. Boy, you'll carry and you'll not speak.

DAVY I can carry.

BARROW You'll carry and you'll not speak.

He stops at the ladder and turns.

BARROW Captain. If she goes over quick, the mat's no good. It wants time.

JUNO Then I'll give you time. Hold her head to the swell and take it slow.

On screen: Barrow (going for'ard without another word), Fen (a long look at Juno, then follows), Davy (following, rag still in his fist)

JUNO Bosun.

BARROW (not stopping) Aye.

JUNO Whatever the court does with you, your ship will have come off that rock with your name on the working of it. I'll write it in the log myself.

Barrow's back is still a moment. Then he lifts one hand, not a wave, only a hand lifted and let fall, and goes on down the ladder.

Meridian Dawn, night

Ambience: a heavy diesel under load, water breaking against a bow

Dark comes down flat and gray. The estuary mouth opens ahead, the bar low and long and pale in the last of the light, the town's roofs beyond it. Rusk's winch has paid out no wire and put no hook on the freighter, and the yellow tug sits off her stern, watching.

On screen: Ines Voss (at the wheel, hands on the worn-bright spokes, eyes for'ard), Juno (at the glass), Teo (in the wheelhouse door, wet to the elbows)

TEO The panel's barred and the gap in the partition's blocked. The pump's led back into her own deep tanks. If she touches, we're pumping her own bilge into her own tanks and laughing about it after.

JUNO Then we'll laugh after.

VOSS Line's taking the strain.

JUNO Take her slow. Hold her head to the swell and let the bosun work the mat.

VOSS She's coming. She's letting go of the rock.

The white water goes out from the bow in a slow ring, and the freighter floats, and she answers her wheel.

VOSS She's off the rock.

JUNO Say that again.

VOSS She's off the rock.

On screen: Juno (taking up the handset), Ines Voss (not looking from the glass), Teo (grinning despite himself)

JUNO Iron Gannet, Ninth Hour. She's off the rock and she's under my tow and she's going into the estuary to be beached on the west flats. Keep your wire off her.

RUSK (on radio) You've not heard the last of this, captain.

JUNO Neither have you.

She sets the handset down. Ahead the bar lies pale across the water, and past it the town's lights have begun to show, small and yellow and very close.

JUNO Take her over slow. Not a touch. Not one.

VOSS Not one.

The freighter's bow comes round to the channel, slow as a held breath, and the town's lights wait beyond the bar.
