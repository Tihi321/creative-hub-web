The Clockwork Compass

Mara Quill

When a young cartographer inherits a brass compass that points not north but toward whatever has been lost, she follows it up the deadliest range on earth. Every answer it gives her costs something — food, fingers, a guide's shoulder — and the truth it is really tracking may be the one thing she cannot carry back down. A cold, exact, quietly triumphant novella about maps, grief, and deciding what to carry home.
