Rust and Ruin

Mara Quill

A condemned tidal foundry. Six days until the wrecking crews arrive. When structural engineer Nadia Voss agrees to a two-hour job retrieving forgotten wartime records, she finds a building that is not simply failing — it is migrating, one tide at a time. To reach the archive hidden in the one room she ruled unsurvivable, she must learn to read the foundry as it truly is, and forgive the mistake that ended her career.
