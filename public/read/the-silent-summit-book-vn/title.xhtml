The Silent Summit

Mara Quill

Ilse Marchetti is deaf, and she reads the ice and the weather through the soles of her boots. When she leads a doubting team up an unnamed seven-thousand-metre peak to recover a lost bronze bell, the weather closes and the radio dies, and the only way down may be a vibration she alone can feel. A story about listening in every sense but the obvious one, and about how no one survives a mountain alone.
