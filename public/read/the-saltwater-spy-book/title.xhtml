The Saltwater Spy

Culo

Marine biologist Isla Renn has spent three months charting the Cobalt Shelf's tidal currents, and she knows them the way she knows her own hands. So when the water in one reef basin stops moving exactly when the tide should be strongest, she knows it isn't instrument error — and following that wrong current leads her to a hidden base sunk into a canyon that appears on no chart. To expose what is really being farmed and smuggled beneath the reef, she will have to recruit a diver who swore never to enter the water again, and time her move to the one tide of the year that can open a door the sea has been holding shut.
