The Winter Garden Clock

Mara Quill

When a collector dies among his frosted orchids with every clock in the house stopped at nine, a sharp-eyed restorer and a talkative locksmith must name the killer before the night runs out. Their only lead is a relic said not to exist, guarded in a Seine-side belfry by three keepers who cannot all be telling the truth. A locked-room puzzle with warm wit and a cold breath of old secrets.
