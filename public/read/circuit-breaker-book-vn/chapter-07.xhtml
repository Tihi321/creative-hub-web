The Ladder

The pumping house under the Silt Market footbridge, night

Ambience: a low steady note with a bad harmonic over it, water running under stone

The footbridge rail ends at a buttress, and the buttress becomes a parapet above the flooded reach. Water pulls fast and brown against the wall.

On screen: Sol (wet to the waist, bag over her shoulder, torch in her teeth), Ife (behind her, line over one shoulder, a loop paid out in her hand), The old man in the vending machine (coat over his head, soaked)

SOL Go back to Mrs. Okafor's. Stand in her window with your lamp and watch the reach.

THE OLD MAN IN THE VENDING MACHINE I am an old fool. I'll stand in a window if it will do any good.

He goes back along the reach with his coat over his head.

Hospital District, night

Ambience: water slapping the district wall, rain coming in flat off the reach

The hospital's floodlights are on because the emergency line held, and the water beneath them is full of reflections, and it is up.

On screen: Sol (on the parapet, listening, knuckles to the stone), Ife (a step behind, one hand where Sol can see it)

SOL The parapet's a foot and a half. Watch the coping.

IFE You'll tell me if the wall's moving.

SOL The wall isn't moving.

IFE The wall will move when it wants to.

Sol crouches where the parapet meets the service stair and puts her knuckles to the stone.

SOL Listen.

IFE I'm listening.

Sound: a low even hum, and over it a thin flutter, tick-tick-tick

SOL The transformer's under that vault lid. Alive. Not running the wards, the emergency line's doing that, up on the upper floors.

SOL It's sitting there waiting to be needed, and the water's on its casing.

IFE How long?

SOL A transformer doesn't promise you. It just gets worse.

IFE Then where's the switch?

SOL The fourth is the pumping house, and it's off and bound and back. But that only takes the market off the fault.

SOL The vault's the hospital's own feed, and the water's over the vault now. There's a link on this wall that separates the vault from the courtyard water.

IFE In his notebook?

SOL No. This one's the one I'm going to make him put in the book.

A squat iron box hangs bolted above the waterline, a grille over its face. The ladder runs down to a landing that has gone under.

SOL Half of it's under. Eight dry rungs, and then the water.

IFE Eight dry rungs and then you're in it to the waist.

SOL Then I'm in it to the waist.

Hospital District, night

Ambience: water slapping the district wall, the clean hum of a works motor

A works boat comes round the corner of the service entrance, an electric boat with a canopy, and its searchlight sweeps the wall and finds them and holds.

On screen: The municipal engineer (high-visibility coat over good rain gear, clipboard in a clear folder, helmet with the works flash), Sol (up on the parapet, turning), Ife (hands still on the line)

IFE Ah. There it is.

The boat comes in to the service stair. The engineer stands up in the bow.

THE MUNICIPAL ENGINEER I'm the municipal engineer of works. Who's in charge here?

SOL I am.

THE MUNICIPAL ENGINEER Name.

SOL Sol Vieira. Apprentice, third year. I'm Dell Marchetti's—

THE MUNICIPAL ENGINEER I know whose you are. It's been on every channel since the pumping house.

THE MUNICIPAL ENGINEER The vault is a live flood hazard and a municipal asset under a standing no-entry provision. The installation you intend to reach is unpermitted, unrecorded and outside the surveyed plant.

THE MUNICIPAL ENGINEER Under section fourteen I'm required to order you to stand down.

Hospital District, night

Ambience: rain on the courtyard water, a handheld hissing low

Heads stand at the hospital's upper windows. A figure waits at the roof door. Across the courtyard a small lamp lifts in a window.

On screen: The woman on the roof (child on her hip, lamp held high), The municipal engineer (folder open), Sol (on the parapet)

THE WOMAN ON THE ROOF I was on a roof in the drowned reach with a crate that was all my boy's food and my medicines.

THE WOMAN ON THE ROOF A bargeman threw me a line and it pulled me straight, and my boy drew breath tonight because of it.

THE WOMAN ON THE ROOF You order that girl down and you order the darkness up. I'm in the window with a light. I'll be here.

On screen: The doctor off the ridge (coat half on, bag in hand, at the top of the service stair)

THE DOCTOR OFF THE RIDGE Thirteen beds. A theatre that stays cold. A blood fridge that has to hold its temperature or the blood in it is water with iron in it.

THE DOCTOR OFF THE RIDGE If the transformer goes, the blood fridge goes with it. I'm telling you what I have under my hands.

THE MUNICIPAL ENGINEER I'm required to order it. Stand down. Withdraw all persons from the hazard.

The courtyard is still. Nobody moves on the parapet.

SOL You can order me down. I can't obey you and keep the wards lit. Both are true and I can't make them fit.

SOL The link on this wall is the last one. If it stays where it is and the fault finds its path, the district goes dark before dawn.

SOL I'm not asking you to say it's legal. I'm asking you to say what's the same either way.

On screen: The old woman at the culvert bridge (waterproof cape, caged work lamp on a cord, sleeves rolled), The sister's boy (canvas tool roll under one arm), The old man in the vending machine (coat over his head, at the back of the parapet)

THE OLD WOMAN AT THE CULVERT BRIDGE Who on this wall remembers the culvert at the depot yard? Because I do.

THE OLD WOMAN AT THE CULVERT BRIDGE There's no section of any code that runs that ladder. The girl runs the ladder. You tell her no and we'll stand here and be the news.

THE MUNICIPAL ENGINEER Who's in charge of that lamp.

THE OLD WOMAN AT THE CULVERT BRIDGE I am.

THE MUNICIPAL ENGINEER And who's in charge of the rest of you?

THE SISTER'S BOY Nobody.

Somebody laughs, and the laugh goes round the courtyard like a stone skipping.

Hospital District, night

Ambience: the handheld opening up, rain easing

On screen: Ozzy (not seen, voice from the handheld), Sol (still on the parapet), The municipal engineer (listening, folder closed on one finger)

OZZY Let me ask the open channel one question, and I want it short. If you are listening and you are inside the hospital district's dark zone, say something.

A NIGHT PORTER A night porter at the south gate. There's nine of us here and two patients on the ground floor, and the corridor's lit and we want it left that way.

AN OLD PERSON AT THE ALMSHOUSES The old folk at the charity almshouses. We have candle. We are not praying for a section number.

MRS. OKAFOR The looter's skiff went up the far reach at the turn of the tide and it's not coming back tonight. The market's dark but the market's people are up.

TOMAS ILYICH Tomas Ilyich. Alive. If it's the engineer's order holding up a switch, I'm the roofer's union and I'll be at the review.

The courtyard is quiet.

SOL That's the district. Thirteen beds and nine at the south gate and nine old people with a candle.

SOL You have a boat and a light and a folder. The vault doesn't care about the folder. It cares about the water.

THE MUNICIPAL ENGINEER I'd have the doctor's word again. Not for the file. For me.

THE DOCTOR OFF THE RIDGE If the transformer goes, a man on my third floor dies of a thing he was going to survive. That's all I'm going to say to a man on a boat.

THE OLD WOMAN AT THE CULVERT BRIDGE That's the first sensible thing said on this wall.

On screen: The municipal engineer (folder shut, looking up at her), Sol (waiting)

THE MUNICIPAL ENGINEER Miss Vieira. I can't authorize the installation.

THE MUNICIPAL ENGINEER The record will reflect that the link is unrecorded and that I found it so, and that the pumping house switch was unrecorded too.

THE MUNICIPAL ENGINEER It will also reflect that I was on the water when I found it, and that I heard the district on the radio.

THE MUNICIPAL ENGINEER I'm not giving you an order to climb. I'm not giving you an order not to. The rest is on your conscience and your mentor's.

THE MUNICIPAL ENGINEER The boat's light is on. I'll hold it steady on the ladder.

SOL On the ladder. Not on me. I want to see the rungs.

THE MUNICIPAL ENGINEER I'll hold it on the ladder.

Sound: the searchlight swinging, a hard click

The beam comes down the wall and steadies, and the rust on the rungs stands out like the veins of a leaf.

Hospital District, night

Ambience: the flood running over the lower rungs, the vault ticking

Sol gets her coat off and bundles it on the coping. She chokes the bag strap up short on her back.

On screen: Sol (at the top of the ladder, bag short on her back), Ife (boots set to the stone, both hands on the stiles), The old man in the vending machine (coat folded over his good arm)

SOL Off first, wait, bind, put it back. The wait's the part that matters.

SOL A switch turned off and straight on finds the fault again. It has to sit off while the water settles.

SOL I'll call it down to you and you'll call it up to Ozzy. If I go quiet, you tell the city I've gone quiet.

IFE Go down. I'm the base, and I don't drop people.

The line comes round Sol's waist once, a bowline, and she checks it with two fingers.

SOL That's not going anywhere.

IFE It was never going anywhere. It's my line.

THE OLD MAN IN THE VENDING MACHINE If I ask you to find a wrench down there you'll laugh at me.

SOL Not tonight. I'll keep my eyes open for the switch.

THE OLD MAN IN THE VENDING MACHINE Then keep your eyes on the switch. I'll hold your coat.

Picture: Sol climbing down the flooded canal face of the Hospital District wall, bag short on her back, the engineer's searchlight holding hard on the rusted rungs below her while Ife braces the ladder above.

The water is at the second rung from the top. Sol puts her boot on the third and goes down into the district's dark.

Hospital District, night

Ambience: the flood running over the lower rungs, the vault ticking

The searchlight holds hard on the rusted rungs. Under the black water at the base of the ladder the vault ticks like a clock asked to hurry.

On screen: Sol (waist-deep on the landing, bag short on her back, hand flat on a squat iron box), Ife (at the top of the ladder, boots set, both hands on the line)

SOL Past the gap. Leave me a fathom to work with.

IFE Leave it.

SOL The tracking's found a path and it's holding it. The casing's burring. It's not hours, and I never said it was hours.

SOL Grille's seized. The box is on a bracket and I don't want to pull the bracket off the wall with a live link in it.

SOL Dell. The grille you put on has seized. Tell me you didn't bolt it through the bracket.

DELL (over the handheld) I bolted it to the box. There's a set screw at the bottom left of the hinge plate. Small, slotted, under the water.

DELL (over the handheld) The hinge plate will be floating. Don't put the driver in and lean on the box.

SOL Then I'm not leaning on the box.

Sol finds the screw with her fingertips and knows at the touch that her driver is too wide.

SOL It's a small slot and my driver's too wide, and if I chew it we're out here with a grille and no bit to fit it.

SOL I can't get the bag off my back with one hand in the water. I need it passed.

SOL A thin blade. A stub wrench, four-inch, for the hinge nut once the screw's out. And a fuse.

OZZY (over the handheld) A fuse. Say it for the city.

SOL I'll not put the link back on a wet fuse.

SOL A cartridge, or fuse wire and a holder, or anything a man in a workshop would have in a tobacco tin.

SOL And copper. Clean strip. When the grille's open the bracket will want a shim or the box will ride on the wall.

SOL I need it now, and not in a boat.

On screen: Sol (hand on the ladder, face tipped into the beam), Ife (turning from the ladder to the parapet)

IFE You heard her. Nobody goes down that wall and nobody stands on the edge to be helpful.

IFE You pass hand to hand and you do not throw. Anything that will sink goes into a hand and stays till another hand has it.

IFE Who's nearest the stair.

THE OLD WOMAN AT THE CULVERT BRIDGE I am.

IFE Then you're first. Boy with the tools, you're second. What's in that roll?

On screen: The sister's boy (canvas roll hugged to his chest, headlamp up), Ife (pointing)

THE SISTER'S BOY Wrenches. Drivers. Pliers. A little torch with a bad switch.

IFE Then you're the shop.

IFE Engineer. You're tall and you've got a light and you've been standing in that boat doing sums. Come here and be the top of my chain.

On screen: The municipal engineer (folder under one arm, helmet off, hair flat to his head)

THE MUNICIPAL ENGINEER I'm not sure it's proper for me to—

THE OLD WOMAN AT THE CULVERT BRIDGE It's proper to hand a wrench to a woman. It's on no section of any code and I've looked.

The engineer's boots come along the parapet. There is a small scuffle and settle of people standing shoulder to shoulder in a wet place and agreeing, without a vote, to be a line.

Picture: Sol far below on the flooded landing of the Hospital District wall, looking up the rusted ladder at a chain of neighbors passing tools hand to hand, the engineer's searchlight pinning them all in one white beam.

What comes down the ladder to her in the dark is a city being a circuit, and it is nothing like the drawings.
