Circuit Breaker

Culo

When a record storm floods Port Alaric's grid control room, seventeen-year-old apprentice electrician Sol Vieira is handed her mentor's notebook and told four buried breakers must be reset by hand before the water reaches the hospital district's emergency line. To cross a city turned to canals she must bargain with a rival bargeman, dive under a live fault, rebuild a looted hub from vending-machine copper, and finally ask her mentor a question he has spent thirty years avoiding. Competence is a form of courage, and no one restores a city alone.
