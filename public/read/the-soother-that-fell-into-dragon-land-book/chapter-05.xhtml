The Bouncing Mud Puddles

The edge of the bouncing mud puddles, Dragon Land, afternoon. Green sky, soft brown puddles that rise and fall like trampolines.

Narration: The puddles bounce up and down with a soft boing, boing, boing. Warm mud squishes under Petra's red boots. Dragonflies hum over the reeds.

On screen: Petra (yellow raincoat, curious), Mango (hopping beside her, satchel bumping)

PETRA They really do bounce. I have never seen a puddle jump before.

MANGO (excited) Everything bounces here! Puddles, clouds, even some of the rocks!

Narration: A puddle bounces extra high. Something brown pops out and lands with a splat.

On screen: Splash (round brown mud dragon, big cheerful grin, drippy wings, tongue out like a happy dog)

SPLASH (joyful) Boing! Hello! I am Splash. I love jumping. Do you love jumping?

PETRA I do! But I am on a very important job.

MANGO (bouncing in place) She lost a soother! Grumble has it in the Golden Cave!

SPLASH (interested) Grumble? Big, sleepy, loves shiny things. Yes, I know Grumble.

PETRA I need something shiny to trade. Nib gave me this little button.

Narration: Petra holds up the button. Splash leans in close and sniffs it.

SPLASH Nice button. Very small. Grumble likes big sparkle.

On screen: Splash (thinking, tapping a muddy claw on its chin)

SPLASH I know! I have a sparkly stone. It fell from the sky. I caught it in a puddle!

PETRA (hopeful) A sparkly stone? Will you trade it?

SPLASH I do not want a button. I want a song. Sing me a song and it is yours!

Narration: Petra thinks. She knows one song. Her grandma sings it at bedtime.

PETRA I know a song about a sleepy moon.

SPLASH (bouncing) Perfect! I love sleepy things! Sing, sing!

Narration: Petra takes a big breath. She sings the sleepy moon song. Her voice is small but brave.

On screen: Petra (singing, eyes closed), Mango (swaying), Splash (eyes wide, hopping gently)

PETRA (singing) The moon went to bed with a yawn and a sigh.

PETRA (singing) The stars tucked it in, way up in the sky.

PETRA (singing) Goodnight, little moon. Sleep tight, little moon.

Narration: The mud dragon stops bouncing. Splash listens with its whole body. Mango wipes a happy tear.

SPLASH (sniffly) That was beautiful. Here! A deal is a deal!

Narration: Splash digs in the mud. The mud dragon pulls out a stone that glows pink and gold.

On screen: Splash (holding out the sparkly stone), Petra (reaching for it), Mango (gasping)

MANGO Ooh, shiny! That is the brightest sparkle I have ever seen!

PETRA (happy) Thank you, Splash! Grumble will love this.

SPLASH Boing! Good luck! Come back and jump with me someday!

Narration: Petra waves goodbye. Splash bounces back into a puddle with a big, muddy splat.

Inside the Golden Cave, late afternoon. Walls sparkle like gold. Piles of shiny treasures everywhere. A big sleepy dragon snores on a heap of gold.

Narration: Petra and Mango tiptoe inside. The light is warm and golden. Coins and cups and keys glitter in heaps.

On screen: Petra (holding the sparkly stone, brave), Mango (close beside her, wings folded), Grumble (huge dusty purple dragon, heavy eyelids, snoring)

GRUMBLE (sleepy rumble) Mmm. Who is in my cave?

PETRA Hello. I am Petra. I brought something shiny.

Narration: Petra holds up the sparkly stone. It glows pink and gold in the warm light.

On screen: Grumble (one eye open, curious)

GRUMBLE Mmm. Sparkle. Let me see it.

Narration: Grumble leans down. He sniffs the stone. His heavy eyelids lift a little.

GRUMBLE Pretty. Very pretty. What do you want for it?

PETRA A soother. A blue round soother. It belongs to my baby brother.

GRUMBLE (blinking slowly) Mmm. I think I have one. Shiny things roll in here all the time.

Narration: Grumble digs in his gold pile. Coins slide. Cups clink. Then he holds up something small and blue.

On screen: Grumble (holding the blue soother between two claws), Petra (eyes wide, hopeful)

PETRA That is it! That is Pip's soother!

GRUMBLE Mmm. Then we trade. Sparkle for soother.

Narration: Petra gives Grumble the sparkly stone. Grumble gives Petra the soother. He puts the stone on top of his gold pile.

GRUMBLE (pleased) Mmm. Good trade. Now I have the prettiest sparkle.

PETRA (happy) Thank you, Grumble! Pip will be so happy!

MANGO (excited) Ooh, shiny! I mean — ooh, soother! We did it, Petra!

Narration: Grumble yawns a big rumbly yawn. Then he smiles a slow, kind smile.

GRUMBLE Mmm. You are far from home, little girl. How will you get back?

PETRA Hmm. My feet are tired. And my boots are full of mud.

GRUMBLE Mmm. Then I will fly you. I am slow, but I am big.

Narration: Petra and Mango climb onto Grumble's warm back. The big dragon steps out of the cave. He spreads his small wings.

On screen: Grumble (spreading wings), Petra (holding the soother tight), Mango (holding Petra's raincoat)

Narration: Whoosh! They swoop up into the green sky. Below them, the mud puddles bounce. The bubble-gum volcano puffs pink.

MANGO (laughing) Wheeee! I found the sky first!

PETRA (laughing) You find everything first, Mango!

Narration: They fly over the tall white mountains. Then Petra sees it. A small cottage with a red door. A clothesline with baby clothes.

PETRA (pointing) There! That is my house!

Narration: Grumble lands softly in the garden. His big feet make two gentle thumps. The flowers nod.

Petra's garden, evening. The sky is soft orange. A clothesline flaps with tiny baby clothes.

On screen: Grumble (landing, sleepy), Petra (hopping down), Mango (fluttering), Pip (in the window, in his green onesie)

Narration: Pip looks out of the window. He sees Petra. He sees the blue round soother in her hand.

PIP (giggling) Ba! Ba ba!

PETRA (running) Pip! I have your soother!

Narration: Petra gives Pip his soother. Pip pops it in his mouth. He smiles a big gummy smile.

On screen: Pip (happy, soother in mouth), Petra (relieved), Grumble (peeking over the fence), Mango (on the fence, proud)

PETRA (warm) There. All fixed. I can fix things.

Narration: Mango opens his satchel. He takes out his shiny button. He gives it to Petra.

MANGO Here. A present. So you remember Dragon Land.

PETRA But Mango, it is your favourite thing!

MANGO (happy) You sang for a stone. That is the best thing of all.

Narration: Petra hugs Mango. He feels warm and a little bit like cinnamon.

GRUMBLE (rumbly) Mmm. I will go home and nap on my new sparkle.

PETRA Thank you, Grumble. Come back and visit any time.

GRUMBLE Mmm. Maybe. If you have snacks.

Narration: Grumble flaps up into the sky. Mango waves goodbye. Then he hops onto the fence.

MANGO I will visit too! I know every way here. I found it first!

Narration: Petra laughs. The garden is warm. The clothesline flaps. Pip giggles with his soother. Everyone is happy.

On screen: Petra (smiling, holding the button), Pip (giggling with his soother), Mango (waving from the fence)

PETRA Sleep well, Dragon Land. See you soon.

Narration: A warm breeze blows one more time. It smells like custard and candy. It feels like a hug.

The End
