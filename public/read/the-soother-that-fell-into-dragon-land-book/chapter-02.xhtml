The Lost Things Office

A path through Dragon Land, morning. The sky is green. Candy-leaf trees lean over a little stone road.

Narration: Petra follows Mango down the road. The ground is soft like a pillow.

Narration: Mango hops ahead. His curly tail swishes. His satchel bumps on his back.

On screen: Petra (walking fast, curious), Mango (hopping ahead, happy)

MANGO Hurry, hurry! The office is just past the custard lake.

PETRA A lake of custard? Real custard?

MANGO Warm custard. It smells like breakfast.

PETRA (giggling) Dragon Land is very silly.

Narration: They pass the lake. It is yellow and steamy. Little dragons paddle in it with spoons.

PETRA Are they eating the lake?

MANGO Only a little. It grows back.

Narration: Ahead is a small round building. A wooden sign hangs by the door.

On screen: A sign that reads "LOST THINGS OFFICE"

PETRA That is a very tidy sign.

MANGO Nib made it. He makes everything tidy.

Inside the Lost Things Office. Pale yellow walls. Wooden shelves full of lost things. A big desk with a lamp and a thick book.

Narration: The office smells like paper and lemon soap. Every shelf is full.

Narration: Hats sit in rows. Keys hang on hooks. One shelf holds only socks.

On screen: Petra (looking around, amazed), Mango (proud), Nib (knee-high green dragon, round glasses, tiny bow tie, holding a label-maker)

NIB (clear) Welcome to the Lost Things Office. Everything has its place.

PETRA Hello. I am Petra. I lost a soother. It is blue and round.

NIB A soother. Let me check the ledger.

Narration: Nib climbs onto a stool. He opens the big book. He runs a claw down the page.

NIB Socks, spoons, hats, one trumpet. Hmm. Let me look further.

PETRA (worried) But it fell over the mountains. It must be here!

NIB (calm) Blue and round, you said? Let me check again.

Narration: Nib turns the page. He taps the ledger with his claw.

NIB Ah. Here it is. Item four hundred and two. Blue soother.

PETRA (hopeful) You found it!

NIB I found the tag. The soother is gone.

PETRA Gone where?

Narration: Mango goes very still. His wings fold down flat. He looks at his feet.

On screen: Mango (sheepish, tail curled), Nib (pointing at Mango), Petra (surprised)

NIB Ask Mango. He brought it in. Then he traded it out.

PETRA Mango? You had my soother?

MANGO (small voice) Ooh. Yes. I found it first. It was so shiny.

PETRA And you traded it?

MANGO For a button. A very shiny button.

Narration: Mango opens his satchel. He shows Petra the shiny button. It winks in the lamp light.

PETRA Where is my soother now?

MANGO (quiet) With Grumble. In the Golden Cave. He loves shiny things.

NIB Grumble guards the cave. He does not give things back for free.

Narration: Petra's heart goes thump, thump. But she is brave. She always fixes things.

PETRA Then I will go and ask Grumble.

NIB Wait. Grumble loves a trade. You should not go with empty hands.

NIB (kind) Everything has its place. And you, Petra, should have something shiny of your own.

Narration: Nib climbs down from his stool. He walks to a small drawer. He opens it.

Narration: Inside is a button. It is big and gold and very shiny.

On screen: Nib (holding up a shiny gold button), Petra (eyes wide), Mango (leaning close)

NIB A lost button. No one came back for it. Take it. Grumble likes shiny things.

PETRA Thank you, Nib! Now I have something to trade.

MANGO (excited) Ooh, shiny! Even shinier than mine!

Narration: Petra puts the gold button in her pocket. It feels warm and lucky.

Narration: Mango's little wings flutter. He wants to help.

MANGO I know the way to the cave. But there are bouncing mud puddles too. Dragons there love to trade.

PETRA (thinking) Hmm. Straight to Grumble, or the mud puddles first?

Narration: Petra looks at the empty shelf where her soother used to sit. Then she looks out the window, at the green sky.

Narration: Somewhere out there, Pip's soother is waiting. She just has to choose her path.

What do you choose?

  • Go straight to the Golden Cave to ask Grumble
  • Visit the bouncing mud puddles to find a trade
