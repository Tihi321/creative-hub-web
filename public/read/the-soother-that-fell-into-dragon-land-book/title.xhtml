The Soother That Fell Into Dragon Land

Culo

When Petra grumpily throws her baby brother's soother over the mountains, a friendly wind whisks her into Dragon Land, where dragons collect lost things and nothing is quite where it should be. To bring the soother home, she must choose her paths, meet giggly new friends, and decide what she truly loves enough to trade. A warm, silly adventure about fixing mistakes and the surprising things we give away.
