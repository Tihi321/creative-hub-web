The Singing Dragons' Chorus

Grassy hill with standing stones, Dragon Land, midday. Green sky, fluffy clouds, flowers that hum.

Narration: Petra follows Mango up a soft green hill. The grass tickles her red boots.

Narration: At the top is a circle of tall grey stones. Dragons sit on each stone, humming.

On screen: Mango (hopping ahead, satchel swinging), Petra (climbing, eyes wide)

MANGO (excited) Here they are! The singing dragons! They know every song about everything.

PETRA They hum like bees. Big, scaly bees.

Narration: The dragons are every colour. One is pink. One is blue. One is tiny and green with a bow tie.

On screen: Nib (little green dragon, round glasses, bow tie, waving from a stone)

PETRA (surprised) Wait. Is that a dragon in a bow tie?

MANGO That is Nib! He runs the Lost Things Office. He sings here on his lunch break.

NIB (clear and tidy) Hello. Welcome to the hill. Please mind the stones. They are older than my ledger.

PETRA I need help. I lost a soother. It is blue and round and it belongs to my baby brother.

NIB A soother. Hmm. Let me think while we sing. Singing helps thinking.

Narration: The dragons take a big breath. The flowers hum along.

On screen: Pink Dragon (mouth open, cheerful), Blue Dragon (swaying), Nib (tapping his foot)

PINK DRAGON (singing) Oh, a soother flew over the mountains so high!

BLUE DRAGON (singing) It bounced off a cloud and it waved goodbye!

PINK DRAGON (singing) It rolled down a hill with a bouncy thump!

BLUE DRAGON (singing) And landed right by a big golden lump!

PETRA A big golden lump? What is a big golden lump?

NIB (singing, tidy and precise) The cave, the cave, with the sleepy old guard.

PINK DRAGON (singing) Who snores very loud and sleeps very hard!

BLUE DRAGON (singing) His name is Grumble and he loves shiny things!

PINK DRAGON (singing) He keeps them all safe under big dragon wings!

On screen: Petra (hands on cheeks, mouth open)

PETRA Grumble has the soother? In the Golden Cave?

NIB (nodding) He does. Grumble guards the cave. He never gives anything back for free.

MANGO (worried) Oh no. Grumble is very big. And very sleepy. And very fond of shiny things.

PETRA Then I need something to trade.

Narration: Nib reaches into his pocket. He pulls out a small round button. It shines like a tiny sun.

On screen: Nib (holding up a shiny button), Petra (leaning in), Mango (eyes huge)

NIB I found this button on the hill. It is lost. No one has asked for it.

MANGO (gasping) Ooh, shiny! I love buttons! I once found a soother and traded it for a button!

NIB (stern) Mango. We do not trade lost things. Everything has its place.

MANGO (sheepish) Sorry, Nib.

NIB (kind) Petra, take the gold button. It may help you trade with Grumble.

PETRA Thank you! But is a gold button enough? Grumble sounds picky.

Narration: The Pink Dragon leans down. Her big pink nose wiggles.

PINK DRAGON (gentle) A button is shiny. But Grumble has forty buttons. I sang that in verse two.

BLUE DRAGON (nodding) He wants something you love. Something that is only yours.

PETRA (thinking) Something I love?

Narration: The Blue Dragon hums a small, soft tune. It sounds like a question.

BLUE DRAGON (kind) Yes. What do you love most in all the world?

Narration: Petra looks at her red boots. At her raincoat. At the leaf in her pocket. Then she smiles.

PINK DRAGON (singing) A boot, a boot, a red rainy boot!

BLUE DRAGON (singing) It jumps in the puddles and gets very muddy!

NIB A muddy boot. Hmm. That is not tidy. But it is true.

MANGO (bouncing) And the mud puddles are full of song! I know the way! I know every way!

Narration: Petra looks at the gold button in her hand. Then she looks down the hill.

Narration: Far away, she can see a cave that glows like gold. Near it, brown puddles bounce up and down like trampolines.

On screen: Petra (thinking, gold button in palm), Mango (hopping), Nib (waiting, gentle smile)

NIB The Golden Cave is straight ahead. The mud puddles are a short hop to the left.

MANGO Either way, I will come with you! I found you first!

PETRA Hmm. The gold button is shiny but small. The puddles might have something better.

Narration: The singing dragons hum softly. A candy-scented breeze gives Petra's hair a gentle nudge.

Narration: Petra holds the gold button tight. She has a big choice to make.

What do you choose?

  • Go to the Golden Cave with the button
  • Go to the bouncing mud puddles to find a better trade
