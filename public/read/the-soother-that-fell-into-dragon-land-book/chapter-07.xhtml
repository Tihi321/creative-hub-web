The Button Trade

Inside the Golden Cave, warm golden light. Stacks of shiny treasures tower like wobbly towers.

Narration: The cave glows like a lantern. Grumble's big claw holds the blue soother.

Narration: Petra holds out something shiny from her pocket. It winks in the golden light.

On screen: Petra (yellow raincoat, hopeful), Mango (peeking from behind her), Grumble (big purple dragon, one eye open)

PETRA Here. This is very shiny. Will you trade?

GRUMBLE (sniffing it) Mmm. A gold button from Nib. I have forty buttons. This one is nice. But it is not special.

PETRA Oh.

GRUMBLE A button is just a button. What's in it for me?

Narration: Petra's shoulders droop. Mango pats her arm with a tiny claw.

On screen: Mango (worried, ears flat), Petra (thinking hard)

MANGO (whisper) I am sorry, Petra. I traded your soother for a button just like it.

PETRA It is okay. You did not know.

Narration: Petra looks down at her feet. Her red rain boots are muddy and wonderful.

Narration: One of them is her favourite. She has worn it on every rainy day.

PETRA (softly) Grumble? What about my boot?

On screen: Grumble (both eyes opening wide), Petra (holding up one red boot), Mango (gasping)

GRUMBLE Mmm. Red. I do not have red.

PETRA It is my favourite boot. I love it very much.

GRUMBLE You love it? Then it is the shiniest thing of all.

Narration: Petra pulls the boot off. She holds it out with both hands.

GRUMBLE (pleased) A red boot. Mmm. Yes. This is special.

SMASH CUT TO Grumble sets the blue soother in Petra's hands and takes the boot.

On screen: Petra (hugging the soother, beaming), Grumble (holding the boot, happy), Mango (jumping)

PETRA Thank you! Thank you, Grumble!

GRUMBLE Mmm. Fair trade. Now I have something red and warm.

MANGO Ooh, shiny boot! I found it first!

GRUMBLE (sleepy smile) No, small dragon. This boot stays with me. I found it this time.

Narration: Grumble hugs the boot close. He tucks it under his big claw.

On screen: Grumble (cuddling the boot), Mango (pouting, then giggling)

MANGO Then keep it safe, Grumble. Keep it very safe.

The green sky over Dragon Land, late afternoon. Fluffy clouds drift by.

Narration: Grumble steps out of the cave. He stretches his big wings.

GRUMBLE Hold on tight, small visitor. I will fly you home.

On screen: Petra (climbing onto Grumble's back), Mango (waving from the ground), Grumble (wings spread)

PETRA You do not have to do that.

GRUMBLE You gave me something you love. I can give you a ride. Mmm.

Narration: They rise into the green sky. Below, the bubble-gum volcano puffs pink bubbles.

PETRA (laughing) I can see the custard lake!

GRUMBLE Wave to the singing dragons.

Narration: Petra waves. The dragons sing a bouncy goodbye song.

Petra's garden, evening. A small cottage with a red door. The sky is soft pink.

Narration: Grumble lands softly in the garden. Petra slides down.

On screen: Grumble (nodding goodbye), Petra (waving), Mango (peeking out of the satchel)

GRUMBLE Goodbye, small visitor. Sleep well.

PETRA Goodbye, Grumble! Goodbye, Mango!

Narration: Grumble flaps up into the sky and is gone. Mango stays, holding his satchel.

Narration: Inside, baby Pip is in his crib. His lip is wobbling.

On screen: Pip (green onesie, sad face), Petra (hurrying in)

PIP (sad) Ba... ba...

PETRA Look what I brought you, Pip.

Narration: Petra holds out the blue soother. Pip's eyes go wide.

PIP (giggling) Goo! Ba!

Narration: He pops the soother in his mouth. He grins a big gummy grin.

PETRA (happy) There. All fixed.

Narration: Pip grins. Then he pulls the soother out and throws it high, high, high!

On screen: Pip (giggling), Petra (mouth open), Mango (laughing)

PETRA Pip! Not again!

MANGO (laughing) Ooh! Here we go again!

Narration: Everyone laughs. Mama comes in and laughs too.

Narration: Mango pats his satchel. It is empty now, but his tail still curls.

MANGO I gave back what I took. That is a good trade too.

PETRA The best trades give something you love.

Narration: Mama picks up the soother and gives it back to Pip.

Narration: Pip giggles and hugs it. The sky outside is pink and warm.

Narration: It was a very silly day. And a very good day.

The End
