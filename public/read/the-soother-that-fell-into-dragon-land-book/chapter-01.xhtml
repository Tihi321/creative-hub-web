The Grumpy Throw

Petra's garden, morning. A small cottage with a red door. Baby clothes flap on a clothesline. The sky is soft blue.

Narration: The sun is warm. Flowers nod in the breeze. It should be a nice day.

Narration: But from the open window comes a sound. A loud, wobbly sound.

On screen: Pip (in a green onesie, red face, crying), Petra (yellow raincoat, hands over ears)

PIP (crying) Waaah! Waaah!

PETRA (grumpy) Pip, shhh! I am trying to build a sandcastle.

PIP (crying) Waaah! Ba! Waaah!

Narration: Petra looks in the window. Pip sits in his crib. His little hands are empty.

PETRA Where is your soother? Oh. It fell under the crib.

Narration: She picks up the soother. It is round, blue, and slobbery. Pip cries louder.

PETRA (grumpy) I have had enough of this soother!

Narration: Petra winds up her arm. She throws the soother high, high, high.

On screen: Petra (eyes wide, mouth open), the soother spinning in the sky

Narration: Up it goes, over the fence. Over the hill. Over the mountains!

PETRA (small) Oops.

Narration: The soother is gone. The garden is quiet. Pip is quiet too.

On screen: Pip (lip wobbling), Petra (hands on cheeks)

PETRA Oh no. That was Pip's favourite thing ever.

Narration: Her cheeks go hot. She wants to fix this. She always fixes things.

PETRA (worried) I can fix this. I just need to go get it.

Narration: Then a warm breeze blows past. It smells like warm custard and candy.

On screen: Petra (hair flying up)

PETRA Whoa! The wind is picking me up!

Narration: The wind lifts Petra off her feet. It carries her up, over the hill, over the tall white mountains.

Dragon Land, near a bubble-gum volcano. The sky is green. Fluffy clouds float by.

Narration: Below her is a tall volcano. It does not spit fire. It spits pink and blue bubbles.

Narration: The bubbles pop with a soft plop. The ground feels like a squishy pillow.

On screen: Petra (landing on her bottom, red boots up)

PETRA Oof! Where am I?

Narration: She stands up. She looks at the green sky. She looks at the gum trees with candy leaves.

PETRA (amazed) This is not home.

Narration: Something small and orange zips past her ear. It lands on a rock.

On screen: Mango (small orange dragon, brown satchel, big amber eyes)

MANGO (excited) Ooh, shiny! A girl! I found her first!

PETRA Hello. Are you a dragon? You are very small.

MANGO I am Mango. I collect lost things. I have a hat, three spoons, and a button.

Narration: Mango opens his satchel. He shows her a shiny button. Then he peers at Petra.

MANGO Are you lost, girl?

PETRA I am Petra. And yes. I lost a soother. It is blue. It is round.

MANGO (head tilt) A so-o-o-ther? What is that?

PETRA It is for babies. My baby brother is very sad without it.

Narration: Mango's tail curls. His amber eyes go wide.

MANGO Lost things are my favourite things! I know where lost things go.

PETRA (hopeful) You do? Where?

MANGO The Lost Things Office. Nib runs it. Everything has a tag and a shelf.

Narration: Petra looks around. The bubble-gum volcano puffs pink. Far away, she hears humming.

PETRA Wait. Who is humming?

MANGO The singing dragons, on the hill. They sing about everything.

PETRA Hmm. Which way should I go?

MANGO (bouncing) Come on, come on! Or we can visit the singers. Either way, we find your thing!

Narration: Mango hops from rock to rock. A custard-scented gust ruffles Petra's hair. It feels like a nudge.

What do you choose?

  • Follow Mango to the Lost Things Office
  • Ask the singing dragons for help first
