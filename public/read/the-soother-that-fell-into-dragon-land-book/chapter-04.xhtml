The Golden Cave

A stone path winding up to the Golden Cave, Dragon Land, afternoon. Green sky, warm golden light spilling from the cave mouth.

Narration: Petra walks up the path. The stones sparkle under her red boots.

Narration: The cave ahead glows like a lantern. A big rumbly snore rolls out of it.

On screen: Petra (yellow raincoat, red boots, eyes wide), Mango (hopping beside her, satchel bouncing)

PETRA That snore is very big. Is Grumble a big dragon?

MANGO As big as a house! But he is mostly sleepy. Sleepy dragons are slow dragons.

PETRA Slow is good. Slow gives me time to be brave.

Narration: They step inside. The walls shine like gold. Piles of shiny things are everywhere.

Narration: Spoons. Keys. One trumpet. A hat with a feather. All stacked in wobbly towers.

On screen: Petra (tiptoeing, mouth open), Mango (whispering, wings low)

MANGO (whisper) Look at all the shiny things. He keeps every one.

PETRA (whisper) Where is the soother?

Narration: Then she sees it. High on a pile, blue and round, sits Pip's soother.

PETRA (gasping) There it is!

Narration: The pile moves. It is not a pile. It is a dragon. He opens one heavy eye.

On screen: Grumble (big dusty purple dragon, heavy eyelids, blinking slowly), Petra (frozen mid-step), Mango (hiding behind her)

GRUMBLE (rumbly) Mmm. A small visitor. And a small dragon. What do you want?

PETRA Hello. I am Petra. That blue soother is mine. Well, it is my baby brother's.

GRUMBLE The soother. Mmm. I like it. It is round and blue and shiny.

PETRA He really needs it. He cries without it. A lot.

GRUMBLE (yawning) What's in it for me?

Narration: Grumble curls his thick tail around the soother. He is not being mean. He is just very fond of shiny things.

On screen: Grumble (sleepy, tail curled around the soother), Petra (thinking hard), Mango (peeking out)

PETRA What do you like?

GRUMBLE Shiny things. Soft things. Things that are mine.

MANGO (quiet) Everything here is his already.

PETRA Then I need something special. Something I really love.

Narration: Petra looks down at her feet. She wears her red rain boots. One of them is her favourite.

Narration: She has worn it on every rainy day. She has jumped in every puddle with it. She loves it very much.

On screen: Petra (looking at her boots, sad smile), Mango (worried), Grumble (one eye open)

MANGO (whisper) Petra, your boot? You love that boot.

PETRA (whisper) I know.

MANGO But it is your favourite!

PETRA Pip's soother is his favourite too.

Narration: Grumble sniffs the air. His big nose wiggles.

GRUMBLE Mmm. What is that smell? It smells like puddles and running.

PETRA That is my boot. My favourite red boot.

GRUMBLE (interested) Red. I do not have red.

Narration: Petra thinks about what she carries. She looks at her boots. She looks at her pocket.

Narration: In her pocket sits the little gold button from Nib. It is small and warm and lucky. Mango still has his own shiny button too.

On screen: Petra (hand near pocket, thoughtful), Mango (holding up his own shiny button), Grumble (peering at both)

MANGO I have my button! The one I traded for the soother. It is very shiny! Very round!

GRUMBLE (sniffing) Mmm. Buttons. I have forty buttons. Your little button is nice. The gold one from Nib is nice too. But I have no red at all.

Narration: Grumble's heavy eyelids lift a little. He looks at the boot. Then at the buttons. Then at the boot again.

GRUMBLE The buttons are small and round. The boot is red and special. You love the boot. I can tell.

PETRA I do love it.

GRUMBLE Then you must choose. What will you trade, small visitor?

Narration: Petra holds Nib's gold button in one hand. She wiggles the toes of her favourite red boot.

Narration: Pip needs his soother. But her boot is hers. This is the biggest choice she has made all day.

On screen: Petra (button in one hand, looking at her red boot), Mango (waiting, tail curled), Grumble (patient, soother held out)

GRUMBLE (rumbly) Mmm. A sleepy dragon has all the time in the world. I can wait.

Narration: The cave is warm and golden. The soother waits in Grumble's claw. Petra's heart goes thump, thump, thump.

What do you choose?

  • Give Grumble the red boot
  • Try to trade the shiny button instead
