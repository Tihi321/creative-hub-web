The Red Boot Trade

Inside the Golden Cave, warm golden light. Stacks of shiny treasures tower like wobbly towers.

Narration: The cave glows like honey. Shiny spoons and keys twinkle on the walls.

Narration: Grumble holds out one big claw. Pip's blue soother sits in it, round and safe.

On screen: Petra (yellow raincoat, holding her red boot), Mango (peeking from behind her), Grumble (sleepy, patient smile)

GRUMBLE (rumbly) Mmm. Take your time, small visitor. I am very good at waiting.

PETRA (soft) It is just a boot. But it is my boot.

MANGO (quiet) You do not have to, Petra. We can think of something else.

PETRA No. I know. Pip needs his soother more than I need a boot.

Narration: Petra pulls off her favourite red boot. She stands on one sock foot. The cave floor is warm.

Narration: She holds the boot up high with both hands.

On screen: Petra (holding the red boot up, standing on one foot)

PETRA Here, Grumble. This is my favourite red boot. It is the thing I love most.

GRUMBLE (eyes wide) Mmm! Red! I have no red at all!

Narration: Grumble takes the boot with his big careful claws. He holds it up to the golden light.

Narration: The red shines. He smiles a slow, rumbly smile.

GRUMBLE It is the shiniest thing in my whole cave. And you gave it to me.

PETRA Yes. Because Pip cries without his soother. And you take good care of shiny things.

GRUMBLE (happy) I will keep it forever. On the tallest pile. Where everyone can see.

Narration: Grumble sets the soother gently into Petra's hands. It is still a little slobbery.

PETRA (grinning) Pip's soother! I did it!

MANGO (bouncing) Ooh, shiny! You got it! You got it!

Narration: Grumble tucks the red boot onto a golden table. He pats it with one claw.

GRUMBLE Mmm. A good trade. The best kind. Now, small visitor, how will you get home?

PETRA Hmm. My feet are tired. And I only have one boot.

GRUMBLE (yawning) Then I will fly you. I am slow, but I am big.

The green sky over Dragon Land, late afternoon. Fluffy clouds drift by.

Narration: Grumble steps out of the cave and stretches his wide wings. They go flap, flap, flap.

Narration: Petra climbs onto his back. Mango zips up beside her, satchel bouncing.

On screen: Petra (riding high, Pip's soother in her pocket), Mango (flying next to her), Grumble (wings spread, rumbling happily)

PETRA Whoa! The bubble-gum volcano looks tiny from up here!

MANGO And there is the custard lake! And the singing dragons on their hill!

PETRA I can see my mountains! The tall white ones!

Narration: A warm breeze blows beside them. It smells like candy and custard.

GRUMBLE (rumbly) Hold tight, small visitor. Here comes the bumpy part.

Narration: They glide over the mountains. Petra laughs the whole way.

Petra's garden, evening. The sky is soft orange.

Narration: Grumble lands softly by the sandbox. The clothesline still flaps with tiny baby clothes.

Narration: Mama and Pip run out of the red door. Pip's face is red and wobbly.

On screen: Petra (jumping down, one red boot), Pip (in a green onesie, crying), Mama (surprised, hands on cheeks), Grumble (sitting politely on the grass), Mango (perched on the fence)

PIP (crying) Waaah! Ba! Waaah!

PETRA Pip! Look! I have your soother!

Narration: Petra kneels down. She holds out the blue soother. Pip stops crying.

Narration: He grabs it with both chubby hands. He pops it into his mouth.

On screen: Pip (soother in mouth, big gummy grin), Petra (kneeling, smiling)

PETRA There. See? I fixed it.

PIP (giggling) Goo! Ba!

MAMA Petra, where have you been? And who are these new friends?

PETRA Mama, this is Grumble. And this is Mango. We made a trade.

MANGO (proud) A very good trade! I found her first!

Narration: Grumble gives a slow, rumbly chuckle. The grass shakes a little.

GRUMBLE She gave me her best red boot. I will keep it forever.

MAMA (smiling) That was very kind of you, Petra. Very kind.

Narration: Pip grins around his soother. His eyes go shiny. He looks at the soother. Then at the mountains.

Narration: He pulls the soother out. He winds up his chubby little arm.

PETRA (eyes wide) Pip. Pip, no.

Narration: Pip throws the soother high, high, high. Over the fence. Over the hill. Over the mountains!

On screen: Petra (mouth open), Pip (giggling), Mama (laughing), Mango (gasping)

PIP (laughing) Ba! Hee hee!

PETRA Pip! Not again!

Narration: Everyone is quiet for one second. Then Mama laughs. Then Mango laughs.

Narration: Grumble rumbles so hard his belly shakes. Petra laughs too, until her tummy hurts.

MANGO (wiping his eyes) Ooh! Now it is lost again! Maybe I will find it first!

PETRA (giggling) Mango, you already have a favourite button. Leave the soother alone.

MANGO No, no! I collect lost things. That is my job. But the boot is for Grumble.

GRUMBLE (nodding) Mmm. And it is the best thing in my cave.

Narration: Grumble lays his big head on the grass. His eyes close. He snores softly.

Narration: Petra sits beside him. Her one red boot is comfy. Her sock foot is a little cold, but she does not mind.

On screen: Petra (sitting on the grass, smiling), Pip (playing with a leaf), Mango (curled on the fence), Grumble (asleep, snoring gently)

MAMA (warm) One boot and one sock are coming in for supper. And so are our guests.

PETRA Mama, do dragons eat supper?

GRUMBLE (half asleep) Mmm. Maybe a little. Something warm.

Narration: Mama brings out a big pot of soup. Grumble sniffs it. His tail thumps.

Narration: Mango sits on the table. He shows Mama his shiny button. She says it is very shiny.

PETRA (happy) Today we traded a boot for a soother. And we made a dragon friend.

MANGO And no one minds a soother flying away. Because things you love are the shiniest things of all.

Narration: The sky goes dark and full of stars. The garden feels warm and safe.

Narration: Pip yawns. He leans on Petra. She hugs him tight, soother and all.

Narration: Somewhere over the mountains, Dragon Land glows like gold. And Grumble keeps the red boot forever.

The End
