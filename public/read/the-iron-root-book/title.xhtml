The Iron Root

Mara Quill

Dr. Naledi Okonjo was barred from fieldwork for telling the truth. Now a village at the mouth of an uncharted valley begs her to find the fever-curing root that grows only in the Iron Basin — and she discovers that pulling it would destroy the one place the plant can live. To save the children, she must learn to read a forest instead of fighting it, and to face a corporate scout who has already signed the valley away on paper.
