Circuit Breaker

Mara Quill

When a record storm drowns Port Alaric's grid control room, seventeen-year-old apprentice electrician Sol Vieira must cross the flooded city on foot to hand-reset the breakers that keep the hospital's lights alive. Armed with her mentor's incomplete notebook and an ear that can hear a bad joint through a wall, she has one night, one barge, and a city of strangers passing tools hand to hand.
