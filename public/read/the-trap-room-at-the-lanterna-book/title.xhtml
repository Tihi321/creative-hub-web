The Trap Room at the Lanterna

Mara Quill

On the last night of the season a vain stage magician drops through his own trapdoor and is found strangled beneath the stage, and a locked door with no key to explain it stands between Inspector Nada Ferluga and the truth. She has until dawn to pin down every alibi, every prop and every lie — and by midnight the theatre's oldest closing-night game begins, in which a Lantern always tells the truth and a Mask always lies. A fair, satisfying puzzle of a mystery set in a gilded, fading variety house by the harbour of Trieste in 1926.
