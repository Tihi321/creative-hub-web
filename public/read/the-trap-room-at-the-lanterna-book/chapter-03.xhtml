Lanterns and Masks

Stage, Sunday, just after midnight

Ambience: rain on the roof, the ghost light humming

The last bell over the harbour fades. The stage is dark except for the ghost light, a bare bulb on a pole that throws long shadows across the boards.

From the wings come three people, one after another, as if they have done this every closing night for years. Each one carries a small paper mask on a stick.

On screen: Teodor (leather apron, iron key ring on his belt, paper mask in hand), Pina (green cardigan, pencil behind her ear, prompt book under her arm, paper mask in hand), Zlatko (striped sailor's jersey, huge rope-burned hands, paper mask in hand)

LUKA (whispering) Who are they?

LIVIA (from her throne, tired) The night crew, darling. Teodor, our carpenter. Pina, our prompter. Zlatko, who works the ropes. They lock up. They keep every key in this house.

NADA Then they are exactly the people I need.

LIVIA Not tonight you don't. Not until dawn.

Sound: three paper masks snapping shut on their sticks

The three of them stop in a line at the edge of the ghost light. Together, without a word, they raise the paper masks to their faces and lower them again.

Stage, Sunday, after midnight

Livia rises from the prop throne. For a moment she is not a tired owner losing money. She is an old actress giving the opening speech.

On screen: Livia (standing, pearls glinting, theatrical), Nada (arms folded, listening), Luka (notebook open), Mira (sitting on the edge of the stage)

LIVIA (grand) It is older than any of us. Older than this roof. On closing night, at the stroke of twelve, the crew draws lots in the paint shop. Some draw a Lantern. Some draw a Mask.

LIVIA Nobody knows who drew what. The paper goes into the stove. And until the sun comes up, they play their part. Every word. To anyone. Even to the police.

Clue: Livia gives the rule as the old company gives it: in the Lanterna game a Lantern always tells the truth and a Mask always lies. Who is a Lantern and who is a Mask?

NADA (dry) And if I arrest them all for obstruction?

MIRA (with a tired grin) They'll go to the cells and keep playing. Teodor once played it through a fire.

LUKA That's... actually rather wonderful.

NADA It is a great nuisance, Luka. Write down that it is a great nuisance.

Nada steps into the circle of the ghost light. She looks at the three of them, one at a time, the way she looked at the trap room.

NADA (counting) One padlocked door. One killer with no key. Three people with keys. And somebody turned one of them in that lock tonight.

Stage, Sunday, after midnight

The crew stand very still. The ghost light buzzes. Somewhere in the flies a rope creaks and settles.

On screen: Nada (in the light, calm), Teodor (masked face lowered, stick in hand, gruff), Pina (prompt book held to her chest, careful), Zlatko (huge hands folded in front of him, solemn)

NADA I will ask each of you the same question. Did you give Doctor Kranjc a key to the trap room tonight?

TEODOR (gruff) No.

PINA (quietly) No.

ZLATKO (slowly) No.

LUKA (whispering, frustrated) Three noes. That's no help at all.

NADA (not taking her eyes off them) It is a great deal of help, Luka. Somebody opened that padlock for him. So one of those three noes is a lie.

LUKA And only a Mask lies.

NADA Only a Mask lies. Find the Mask, and you find the key.

Kranjc, in the shadow of the wings, gives a soft cough. It might be a laugh.

KRANJC (polite) Charming. A parlour game for a murder. I did warn you, Inspector.

NADA You did, Doctor. Be quiet now, please. I am counting.

Stage, Sunday, after midnight

Nada turns her back on Kranjc. She takes Luka's notebook out of his hands and opens it to a clean page.

On screen: Nada (notebook open, pencil ready), Pina (prompt book under her arm, very precise), Teodor (frowning under his white stubble), Zlatko (patient, enormous, in the edge of the light)

NADA One line each, then. Not about yourselves. About the others. Tell me what you know about your crew. Signorina Sardo, you first.

Pina opens her prompt book, as if she might find her line written there. She closes it again.

PINA (exact, quiet) Then I say this, and I will not say it twice.

Clue: Pina says: "At least one of Teodor and Zlatko is a Mask."

LUKA (writing furiously) "At least one of Teodor and Zlatko." At least one. Could be both.

NADA Could be. Signor Marušić.

Teodor scratches his stubble. He looks at Zlatko beside him, then back at Nada, and shrugs one heavy shoulder.

TEODOR (gruff) Easy, that. I'll say it straight.

Clue: Teodor says: "Zlatko and I are both Lanterns."

LUKA Both of them. Him and Zlatko. Both Lanterns.

NADA And Signor Horvat.

Zlatko takes a long time. He looks at the boards, at his own rope-burned hands, and at last at the small woman with the prompt book.

ZLATKO (slow, deep) I only know one thing for sure, Inspector.

Clue: Zlatko says: "Pina is a Lantern."

The ghost light hums. Nobody on the stage moves. Even Kranjc has stopped smiling.

LUKA (whispering) Three lines. That's all we get?

NADA (closing the notebook) That is all we need.

Picture: Nada stands in the circle of the ghost light on the bare stage, facing Teodor, Pina and Zlatko in a line with their paper masks lowered, while Luka watches from behind her.

Puzzle: Lanterns and Masks

  • In the Lanterna game a Lantern always tells the truth and a Mask always lies. Who is a Lantern and who is a Mask?
  • Pina says: "At least one of Teodor and Zlatko is a Mask."
  • Teodor says: "Zlatko and I are both Lanterns."
  • Zlatko says: "Pina is a Lantern."

NADA (quietly) Signora Sardo is a Lantern. Signor Horvat is a Lantern. And you, Signor Marušić, are the Mask.

On screen: Teodor (very still, hand closing over his key ring), Nada (calm), Pina (eyes down), Zlatko (sad, steady)

LUKA (working it through out loud) If Pina were a Mask, then her line would be a lie, and both of the others would be Lanterns. Then Zlatko tells the truth, and says Pina is a Lantern. But she isn't. So she can't be a Mask. So she's a Lantern.

NADA Go on.

LUKA So her line is true. One of Teodor and Zlatko is a Mask. And Zlatko says Pina is a Lantern, which is true, so... Zlatko's a Lantern too. So the Mask is...

He stops. He looks at the old carpenter with the ring of keys.

LUKA (quietly) So Teodor's line was a lie. He isn't a Lantern at all.

NADA Which makes his "No" a lie too, Luka. He gave Doctor Kranjc the key.

TEODOR (gruff, lifting his chin) I've never seen that doctor before in my life.

PINA (very softly) He has. Every night this month. He brings him cigarettes.

ZLATKO (slowly) He went down the trap room stairs with the doctor at ten to ten, Inspector. I saw them from the flies. I did not know why. I am sorry, Teodor.

Teodor's hand tightens on his key ring until the iron rattles. He does not look at Kranjc. He does not look at anyone.

TEODOR (gruff, to the floor) You've got it all wrong. I'm a happy man. I owe nobody nothing.

NADA (gently) Until dawn, then, Signor Marušić. I can wait until dawn.

In the wings, Kranjc picks up his black bag. A constable steps into the light beside him, and he puts the bag down again.

NADA You will wait too, Doctor. We all will. The sun is not in a hurry, and neither am I.
